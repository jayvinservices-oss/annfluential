# Northbook Finance: first prospect list and marketing plan (v1, 5 Nov 2026)

For Jayvin Consulting Inc. (working brand Northbook Finance; see `jayvin/finance-practice-plan.md`). Offer: AI-assisted
bookkeeping and board-ready finance reporting for small nonprofits, charities and small businesses, reviewed and signed
off by Ann (CA (ICAN), MBA; finance director background).

Companion file: `jayvin/research/prospects.csv` (same 40 prospects, one row each, with a `status` column to work from).

**Before any outreach.** The plan's guardrails still apply: read the employment contract (outside work,
non-solicitation, conflicts), keep everything off employer time, devices and contacts, have the engagement letter and
E&O insurance in place, and use the practice email only.

## How this list was built

- **Source of figures:** CRA "List of charities" open data, 2024 release (T3010 returns with fiscal years ending in
  2024), from [open.canada.ca](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/80c00cdb-1358-415c-bb8b-0de7f12675b8). Revenue is
  T3010 line 4700 (total revenue). Staff counts are Schedule 3 lines 300 (permanent full-time) and 305 (part-time).
  Growth compares against the 2022 release (FY2022 returns). Each prospect links to its CRA public listing.
- **Filters:** registered charities, designation C (charitable organisations, not foundations), GTA and nearby
  (Toronto, Peel, York, Durham, Halton); revenue CA$250k to CA$3M; 5 or fewer full-time staff; religious
  congregations, schools, hospitals and foundations removed; must have a working website. 439 charities passed the
  data filters. They were scored on revenue in the CA$400k to 2.5M sweet spot, 1 to 4 full-time staff (enough
  activity, no room for a finance hire), growth since FY2022, cash-basis filing and community-service mission. The top
  ~50 had their websites checked; 39 made the list, plus 1 nonprofit found through a public Treasurer posting.
- **Website check:** home, contact, careers and about/team pages were read for a general contact route, any finance
  staff titles (Finance Manager, Director of Finance, Controller, Finance Coordinator, Bookkeeper) and mentions of a
  board Treasurer. Only general addresses (info@, hello@, contactus@) and contact pages are recorded. No personal
  emails or names were collected.
- **Job-board signal:** public postings found by search for part-time bookkeeper or finance roles at GTA charities.
  Most hits were churches, large agencies or out of range. Prologue Performing Arts (on this list) posted a part-time
  Finance Coordinator role. Charity Village and JobLeads blocked direct reading from this session, so a manual
  Charity Village search is item 1 of week 2 below.
- **Tiers (from the jayvin-prospect-researcher method):** **Hot** = small team, no finance staff visible, fast growth
  or volunteer-run. **Warm** = mid-size or unclear finance capacity, or already has a finance person (pitch board
  reporting or fractional finance director, not bookkeeping). No Cold leads were kept.
- **Exclusions applied:** Roots of Empathy, DNC, QT Literary Magazine / Queer Toronto Literary Magazine and Rainbow
  Railroad are not on the list. Foundations and school boards were filtered out, which removes the likeliest
  funders and partners of Ann's employer. Ann should still run the 40 names past her own knowledge of her employer's
  funders, partners and vendors before contacting anyone. Prologue works with school boards, so it is flagged for that
  check.
- **Not checked:** late or qualified filings. The open data does not show filing dates or audit opinions. The CRA
  listing page for each charity shows whether the latest return is filed; a quick look before contacting is
  worthwhile, since a "return not filed" status is a strong hook.

Caveats: FY2024 is the latest year in the open data, so figures are one to two years old. Some sites blocked the
automated check (noted in the contact route). Staff counts are as reported by the charity and are sometimes left
blank.

## 30-day outreach plan

Assumes the week-1 and week-2 launch steps in the practice plan (name, domain, practice email, engagement letter,
E&O) are done or under way. Ann's time: about 4 to 5 hours a week.

**Week 1: set up and first ten**
1. Check the 40 names against Ann's own knowledge of her employer's funders, partners and vendors; strike any overlap.
2. Glance at the CRA listing for each Hot prospect: note any "return not filed" status.
3. Start QuickBooks ProAdvisor certification; set up Google Business Profile and the Northbook Finance LinkedIn page.
4. Build a one-page offer PDF (three packages, prices, what Ann signs off).
5. Send email A to the first 10 Hot prospects. Log each in the CRM sheet (`status` column).

**Week 2: widen the net**
1. Manual Charity Village and Goodwork search for GTA part-time bookkeeper, finance coordinator and volunteer Treasurer
   postings. Add any in-range organisation to the list.
2. Send email A to the other 8 Hot prospects and 5 Warm ones.
3. Personal note to 25 people in Ann's own network.
4. List 15 small audit firms and 10 nonprofit consultants; send email B to 5 of each.
5. Follow up once with week-1 contacts who have not replied (a two-line "following up" note).

**Week 3: give something useful**
1. Publish the first two articles (titles 3 and 2 above). Link the board-report template from each.
2. Announce a free 45-minute webinar for week 4: "Your first board finance report". Invite every prospect on the list,
   the network contacts and Toronto Neighbourhood Centres (ask if it will share with member centres).
3. Send email A to the remaining Warm prospects.
4. Follow up once with week-2 contacts.

**Week 4: convert**
1. Run the webinar; record it for the website.
2. Offer every attendee a free 30-minute finance check (books, board pack, T3010 readiness).
3. Book discovery calls; send proposals within two days of each call.
4. Pilot offer from the plan: Board Ready at CA$1,150/month for 3 months in exchange for a testimonial and a referral.
5. Review: replies, calls, proposals. Keep the channels that produced a call; drop the rest.

**Targets for day 30:** 60 contacts reached, 8 to 10 replies, 4 to 6 discovery calls, 2 proposals, 1 pilot signed.
