# Brand names and domains: AI-driven full finance support

Prepared 5 November 2026 for Jayvin Consulting Inc. (Ontario). The brand will be a registered business (trade) name of the corporation.

Service: bookkeeping, monthly close, board-ready reporting and a fractional finance director for small nonprofits and small businesses in Canada and the US. AI does the work; a human finance director reviews every decision.

### Which words carry intent

1. **Outsourced bookkeeping / outsourced accounting**: highest buyer intent (CPC US$36-64). Use in page titles, not the brand.
2. **Fractional CFO**: the volume leader for advisory work. "Fractional finance director" gets almost no searches, so say "fractional CFO / finance director" on the site.
3. **Nonprofit / not-for-profit**: Canadians write "not-for-profit" and "charity"; Americans write "nonprofit". Use both on pages.
4. **Small business**: a modifier on almost every buyer query.
5. **AI bookkeeping**: real, growing demand (about 3,000 a month across variants), but it leans toward software. It works as a differentiator, not a lead keyword.
6. **Board / treasurer**: low volume but highly specific to the nonprofit buyer ("treasurer report", "board financial report template"). These are good content-marketing topics.
7. **City modifiers** (Toronto, Canada) matter for local SEO. Use them in page titles and Google Business Profile, not in the brand.

**SEO conclusion:** exact-match domains give little ranking lift today. Rankings come from page titles ("Outsourced Bookkeeping & Fractional CFO for Nonprofits | Brand"), content and links. A memorable, trademarkable brand plus keyword-rich pages beats a keyword-stuffed domain. A name that contains a real intent word (books, finance, board) still helps click-through and anchor text.

## 4. Recommendation: top 3

### 1. Books to Board (bookstoboard.com / .ca / .ai, all likely available)
- **Says "full finance support"** without saying it: from the books to the board pack. Treasurers and EDs recognise "board" at once, and small-business owners read "board" as the owner's or lender's view.
- **SEO:** contains "books" (the bookkeeping cluster) and "board" (board reporting, treasurer). Page titles carry "outsourced bookkeeping", "fractional CFO" and "nonprofit".
- **AI signal:** comes from the descriptor, not the name. That keeps it from sounding gimmicky and future-proofs it when "AI" stops being a differentiator. Suggested lockup: **Books to Board: AI-driven finance, reviewed by a finance director.**
- **Domains:** a three-way clean set is rare. Buy all three. Use **bookstoboard.com** as the primary site (neutral and trusted on both sides of the border); point .ca and .ai at it. Use bookstoboard.ai in AI-themed campaigns if wanted.
- **Registrability:** no conflicts found; distinctive enough for a trade name and probably for a trademark (a coined phrase, not purely descriptive).

### 2. Countersign Finance (countersignfinance.com / .ca / .ai, all likely available)
- **Tells the AI-plus-human story in one word:** the AI prepares, the finance director countersigns. That is exactly the trust promise a treasurer wants.
- **SEO:** "finance" is a broad intent word; weaker on "bookkeeping" than option 1.
- **Risk:** Countersign is an e-signature software brand. Different service, but check CIPO and USPTO classes 9, 35, 36 and 42 before committing.
- **Memorability:** high, and premium-sounding. It suits the fractional finance director end of the offer.

### 3. BoardReady Finance (boardreadyfinance.com / .ca / .ai, all likely available)
- **Most nonprofit-specific:** "board-ready reporting" is the deliverable the brief names, and it echoes "board financial report" searches.
- **Risk:** "board ready" is descriptive, so the mark is harder to protect, and BoardReady.ai and AACUC BoardReady already exist. It is fine as an Ontario trade name, weaker as a trademark.
- Reads less naturally for small businesses than for nonprofits.

**Honourable mention:** **AI Finance Director** (aifinancedirector.ai / .ca available). It is the most literal match for the brief, good for paid search and as a product or service-line name ("Books to Board AI Finance Director"), but too descriptive to own as a brand.

### .ai vs .ca vs .com
- **.ca** signals Canadian and local, which matters for nonprofits funded by Canadian grants, and Google geotargets it to Canada. It requires Canadian presence; an Ontario corporation qualifies. Costs about C$15-25 a year.
- **.ai** signals tech. Google treats it as a generic TLD, so it has no Anguilla geotargeting. Some older treasurers and board members still find it unfamiliar or "startup-y", and small charities are risk-averse about where money data goes. It costs about US$70-100+ a year, and most registrars require a **2-year minimum** term (about US$150-200 up front), plus renewal-price creep.
- **.com** is the default trust anchor for a Canada and US audience. Where the .com is free (all three picks), make it primary and hold .ca and .ai as redirects. If Jayvin wants to lead with AI, use the .ai in campaigns only.

### Ontario trade-name rules
- Register under the Business Names Act through ServiceOntario: C$60 online, 5-year term. Show "Jayvin Consulting Inc." alongside the trade name on contracts, invoices and the site footer.
- **Avoid** "CPA", "Chartered Professional Accountant", "Chartered Accountant", "Public Accountant", "Audit"/"Assurance", and anything implying a licensed public accounting firm (CPA Ontario protects these under the Chartered Professional Accountants of Ontario Act, 2017). None of the top 3 does.
- Avoid "Bank", "Trust" and "Financial Planner" (regulated in Ontario under FSRA title protection). "Finance" and "Books" are fine.
- Add a footer line such as "Bookkeeping and financial management services; not a public accounting firm. Audits and review engagements are referred to licensed CPA firms." It protects the brand and reassures boards.

1. Confirm and buy bookstoboard.com, .ca and .ai at one registrar (and countersignfinance.com as a hold if undecided).

3. Run an Ontario business-name search, then register the trade name.
