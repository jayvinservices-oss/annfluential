## 2. Monthly plans (CA$, plus HST), with stated hours
Hours are the finance director's included time each month (review, calls, analysis). Routine processing done by the
AI system does not count against them. Extra work: **CA$150/hour**, billed in 15-minute steps, approved before it
starts. Unused hours do not roll over. Rush turnaround under 2 business days: plus 25%.

| Plan | For | Includes | Price | Included hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | Owner-run businesses, up to about 150 transactions a month | Categorising and matching, all reconciliations, monthly close by day 10, P&L and balance sheet, receipts chased, HST working papers, year-end package for the accountant | **$750/month** | 4 h |
| **Board Ready** | Businesses with partners, investors or a board; nonprofits | Essentials plus a monthly management pack (budget vs actual with notes, cash position, 13-week cash forecast), class or fund tracking, quarterly review call | **$1,500/month** | 8 h |
| **Fractional CFO** | Organisations without a finance lead | Board Ready plus annual budget and reforecast, one board or finance committee meeting a month, audit or review preparation, controls and policies, pricing and hiring decisions support | **$3,000/month** | 16 h |

Terms: monthly in advance; 3-month minimum, then 30 days' notice. The client owns its QuickBooks file and data at all
times; on exit we hand over the file, a closing reconciliation and the handover pack.
Guarantee: books closed by the 10th working day, or that month's fee drops by 25%.

## 3. One-time implementation fee (required before monthly work)
Paid 50% at signing, 50% at go-live. First two clients get 50% off in exchange for a written case study.

| Package | Price | What is set up | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Essentials setup** | **$1,500** | QuickBooks Online review and clean-up of the chart of accounts, bank and card feeds, bank rules, AI categorisation rules, document inbox, opening balances checked, month-end checklist | 2 to 3 weeks |
| **Board Ready setup** | **$3,000** | Essentials setup plus the management pack built on the client's numbers, budget loaded, class or fund tracking, 13-week cash forecast model, AI Use Policy adopted | 3 to 4 weeks |
| **Finance Office setup** | **$5,000** | Board Ready setup plus 3 AI workflows built and documented (for example bills and approvals, receipts, reporting), controls review, finance policies, 7-template handover pack | 4 to 6 weeks |

**Finance health check, $1,000**, credited in full against any setup if the client signs within 30 days: read-only
review of the books, controls and reporting, a 2-page findings note and a fixed quote.

## 4. Clean-up (catch-up) jobs: fixed price, fixed delivery
Price after a free 30-minute call and a read-only look at the books:
- **$300 per month of backlog** (up to about 150 transactions a month), **$450** for complex months (payroll, several
  bank or card accounts, inventory, restricted funds). Minimum $1,500. 50% up front.
- Delivery: up to 6 months of backlog in 4 weeks; 7 to 12 months in 6 to 8 weeks.

What the client receives at sign-off:
1. A QuickBooks file categorised and reconciled to the latest month-end.
2. Reconciliation reports for every bank, card and loan account, each tied to a statement.
3. Monthly P&L and balance sheet for the period, and a trial balance.
4. A list of adjusting entries made, with reasons.
5. An open-items list (missing receipts, unknown transactions, questions for the accountant).
6. A year-end package ready for the external accountant.
7. A 30-minute walkthrough call.

Clients who move to a monthly plan within 30 days get 50% off the implementation fee.

## 6. Revenue and capacity
- **While employed (2 to 3 clients):** for example 1 Fractional CFO + 1 Board Ready + 1 Essentials = **$5,250/month**
  (28 included hours, about 7 h/week), plus implementation fees ($6,500 to $9,500 one-time) and digital products.
- **About $10,000/month:** 2 Fractional CFO + 2 Board Ready + 2 Essentials = $10,500/month (56 included hours, about
  14 h/week). Realistic once Ann has more time, or with a part-time bookkeeper on Essentials work.
- Market check (competitor notes): human-reviewed AI bookkeeping US$300 to 850/month; Enkel (closest Canadian rival)
  nonprofit tiers CA$800 to 1,250; Toronto fractional CFOs CA$1,500 to 12,000/month; catch-up US$150 to 500 per month
  of backlog. Our prices sit mid-market with a stronger senior-review story.
