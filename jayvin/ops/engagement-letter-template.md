## 1. Who we are and how we work

Jayvin is an Ontario corporation that provides bookkeeping, management reporting, budgeting and finance advisory services. ## 2. Services and scope

You have chosen the **{{plan}}** plan. The services in each plan are listed below. Only the plan named above applies.

**Essentials**
- Categorising and matching bank, card and loan transactions in QuickBooks Online.
- Reconciling every bank, card and loan account to its statement each month.
- Closing the month by the 10th working day.
- A monthly profit and loss statement and balance sheet.
- Chasing missing receipts.
- HST working papers for you to file.
- A year-end package for your external accountant.

**Books to Board**

- A monthly management pack: budget against actual with notes, cash position and a 13-week cash forecast.

- A quarterly review call.

**Fractional CFO**
- Everything in Books to Board.
- An annual budget and a reforecast during the year.

- Preparation support for your audit or review engagement.
- Financial controls and finance policies.
- Support with pricing and hiring decisions.

**Included hours.** Your plan includes **{{included_hours}} hours** a month of Ann's time for review, calls and analysis. Routine processing done by our AI system does not count against these hours. Unused hours do not roll over to the next month.

**Extra work.** Work beyond your plan is charged at **CA$150 an hour**, billed in 15-minute steps. We will tell you the estimated hours and get your approval in writing (email is fine) before any extra work starts. Rush turnaround of less than 2 business days adds 25%.

## 3. What is out of scope

The following are not part of this engagement. Where you need them, we are glad to help you find the right professional.

- **Assurance engagements.** We do not perform audits, reviews or compilation engagements (including Notice to Reader reports), and nothing we provide is an assurance report. Your external accountant or auditor is responsible for those.
- **Legal advice.** We do not give legal advice.
- **Tax advice.** Our tax work is limited to preparation support, such as HST working papers and year-end packages for your accountant. Tax planning, tax opinions and representation before the Canada Revenue Agency or other authorities are outside this engagement unless agreed separately in writing.
- **Filing and paying on your behalf.** We do not file returns or make payments or remittances for you. You file and pay your own HST, payroll, corporate, information and other returns and remittances.
- **Holding your money or logins.** We never hold your bank logins, sign cheques or move funds.

## 7a. Documents we verify against

**Verification documents (you provide these before setup starts):**
- Bank, credit card and loan statements for every account: the last 3 months, plus every month being caught up for a clean-up job, plus the statement at the last fiscal year end.
- The most recent year-end financial statements: audited, review engagement or compiled (Notice to Reader), with the auditor's management letter if there is one. For organisations with none: the year-end trial balance and the last corporate tax return (T2) or charity return (T3010).
- The last filed HST return and notice of assessment, and payroll remittance statements (PD7A) if there is payroll.
- Loan and lease agreements, grant and contribution agreements, and the approved budget for the year.
- Supporting schedules where they exist: fixed assets, deferred revenue or restricted funds, accounts receivable and payable listings.

**How we use them:**
1. Opening balances: agree the QuickBooks balances at the last year end to the year-end financial statements; list and resolve every difference before the first close.
2. Cash: agree every bank, card and loan balance to its statement at year end and at each month end since; every reconciliation is tied to a statement, never to a balance someone typed in.
3. Liabilities and assets: agree HST, payroll remittances, loans and leases to the statements and agreements; fixed assets and deferred or restricted amounts to their schedules.
4. Income: match grants and contracts to their agreements (amount, period, conditions); check that restricted money is tracked as restricted.
5. Spending: sample bills and expenses back to invoices and receipts; anything without support goes on the open-items list.
6. Report: a short verification note to the client listing what was checked, differences found and what was adjusted, with the client's sign-off before monthly work begins.
This is verification for bookkeeping and reporting, not an audit or review.

We rely on the documents and explanations you give us. If something cannot be verified, we tell you in writing and it stays on the open-items list until it is resolved; we do not guess.

## 8. Ownership of books and data

**Your organisation owns its QuickBooks file, its records and all of its data at all times.** We access them only to provide these services. When the engagement ends, for any reason, we will:

- hand over full control of the QuickBooks file (we remove our own user access once you confirm the handover);
- provide a closing reconciliation of every bank, card and loan account to the last completed month-end; and
- provide the handover pack: the chart of accounts, bank rules, month-end checklist, open-items list and any working papers we prepared for you.

We do not hold your records back because of an unpaid invoice.

We keep our own working papers and templates, which remain ours. We may keep a copy of your records for as long as professional and legal record-keeping rules require, after which we delete them securely.

## 9. Confidentiality

We keep your information confidential. We use it only to provide these services and share it only with your permission, with the service providers listed under section 11, or where the law requires. This continues after the engagement ends.

## 10. Privacy

We handle personal information (for example employee, customer or donor details in your books) in line with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). In particular:

- we collect and use personal information only as needed to provide these services;
- we protect it with reasonable safeguards, including multi-factor authentication, a password manager with a separate vault for each client, and access limited to the people working on your file;
- we tell you promptly if we become aware of a breach of security safeguards involving your information, so that you can meet your own reporting duties; and
- you confirm that you have the authority to share the personal information in your records with us for this purpose.

## 11. Use of AI

We use AI tools to make the work faster and more consistent. We want you to know exactly how.

- **Tools used:** we currently use Claude (Anthropic), on Jayvin's business plan, connected to your QuickBooks Online file through the QuickBooks connector, together with the AI features built into QuickBooks Online itself. We will tell you before adding a new AI tool that touches your data.
- **Human review:** a person reviews every AI output before it is used. No transaction is posted to your books, and no report is sent to you, without Ann's review and approval.
- **No training on your data:** we use only business plans whose terms say that customer data is not used to train the provider's AI models. Your data is not used to train AI models.
- **Your data stays in your systems:** your books stay in your own QuickBooks file. The AI tools read and work with your data through connections that you can revoke at any time. Each client has its own separate workspace, and your data is never mixed with another client's.
- **Service providers:** a list of the service providers (subprocessors) that may process your data, and where they process it, is available on request.
- **Your AI policy:** if your organisation has an AI policy, we will follow it. If it does not, and your plan includes one, we will help you adopt one.
