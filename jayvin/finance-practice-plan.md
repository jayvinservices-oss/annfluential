# Jayvin finance practice: offer, pricing and plan (v1, 5 Nov 2026)

Working brand: **Northbook Finance**, a service of Jayvin Consulting Inc. (Ontario corporation, sole director Ann
James Nweke). Brand name is a placeholder; alternatives below. This is the "JV Consulting" line in the Jayvin ledger.
Website: hidden draft in `jayvin/site/` (not live). Status: planning, no clients signed under this offer yet.

## 1. What we sell, in one line
Monthly bookkeeping and board-ready finance reporting for small nonprofits and small businesses, run on an
AI-assisted system and signed off by an experienced finance director.

Why this and not content: it is recurring revenue, it uses what Ann already does at director level, and AI does most
of the routine work (categorising, reconciling, drafting reports) while Ann reviews and signs. $10k a month is 6 to 10
clients, not a viral moment.

## 2. Pricing, anchored to Ann's time
Ann's salary is CA$130,000. As an employee that is about CA$62/hour (2,080 hours) before benefits and overhead.
A consultant has unbilled time (sales, admin) and no benefits, so the usual rule is to bill about 2 to 2.5 times the
salary-hour.

- **Ann's hourly rate: CA$150/hour** (advisory, catch-up work, anything outside a package). Floor CA$125.
- Packages are priced on value and on the AI doing the routine part; Ann's effective rate inside them is higher.

| Package | For | Includes | Price (CA$, + HST) | Ann's time/month (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Books** | Small business or nonprofit, up to about 150 transactions/month | Categorisation, bank and card reconciliations, monthly close, P&L and balance sheet, receipts chased, year-end package for the accountant | 650/month | 3 to 4 h |
| **Board Ready** | Nonprofits that report to a board or funders | Everything in Monthly Books, plus a board finance pack (budget vs actual with notes, cash position, 3-month cash forecast), restricted funds tracking, funder report schedule | 1,450/month | 7 to 9 h |
| **Fractional Finance Director** | Organisations without a finance lead | Everything in Board Ready, plus annual budget, finance committee meeting (1/month), audit preparation and auditor liaison, policies and controls advice | 2,950/month | 14 to 18 h |

One-off work:
- Catch-up bookkeeping: CA$150/hour, or a fixed quote per month of backlog after a free 30-minute look.
- Annual budget build: from CA$1,800.
- Audit preparation (year-end file, schedules, auditor requests): from CA$2,400.
- Finance policies and procedures manual: from CA$1,500.
- Charity information return (T3010) preparation support: from CA$650 (Ann prepares; the charity's director signs and files).

Terms: monthly in advance, 3-month minimum then month to month, 30 days' notice. Payment by e-transfer or card.
Prices reviewed each January.

**Path to about CA$10k/month (example mix):** 2 Fractional (5,900) + 2 Board Ready (2,900) + 2 Monthly Books (1,300)
= CA$10,100/month for about 55 to 65 hours/month (13 to 15 h/week), an effective CA$155 to 185/hour.
A lighter mix: 5 Board Ready + 2 Monthly Books = CA$8,550/month for about 45 h/month.

## 3. Market and scope
Estimates below are approximate public figures; verify before quoting them anywhere.
- **Canada:** about 86,000 registered charities and roughly 170,000 nonprofits in total, most with small budgets and
  no in-house finance staff. About 1.2 million employer small businesses.
- **Not Canada-only.** Bookkeeping, management reporting, budgeting and finance-director advisory are not regulated
  professions and can be delivered remotely to US clients too (the US has well over 1.5 million nonprofits).
  Tax filings are where it gets jurisdiction-specific: in the US do bookkeeping and reporting only and leave tax
  returns to a US preparer.
- **Start narrow:** Ontario nonprofits and charities with budgets of CA$250k to CA$3M, then small service businesses
  (agencies, clinics, consultants). Nonprofits refer each other, and board reporting is the gap they feel most.
- **Excluded from prospecting:** DNC (Ann chairs its finance committee), QT (existing client), Ann's employer and
  anyone she deals with through her job (funders, partners, vendors).

## 4. Legal and professional guardrails (pointers, not legal advice)
- **Entity:** invoice as Jayvin Consulting Inc. A trading name such as "Northbook Finance" must be registered in
  Ontario under the Business Names Act (online, about CA$60, renew every 5 years). Contracts and invoices show both:
  "Northbook Finance, a service of Jayvin Consulting Inc."
- **Scope limits:** no audits, reviews or compilation (Notice to Reader) engagement reports. Public accounting is a
  licensed activity in Ontario; check CPA Ontario's rules before offering any engagement report. Ann's work products
  are management reports, not assurance.
- **Titles:** use only designations actually held, exactly as granted. Do not use "CPA" or "Chartered Professional
  Accountant" unless licensed.
- **HST:** register once revenue passes CA$30,000 in four consecutive quarters (or voluntarily earlier to claim input
  tax credits).
- **Insurance:** professional liability (errors and omissions) before the first paying client under this offer.
- **Engagement letter for every client:** scope, what the client is responsible for (source documents, approvals,
  filing and paying), limitation of liability, data handling, AI use disclosure (tools used, data not used to train
  models, human review of all outputs).
- **Ann's job:** read the employment contract for outside-work, conflict of interest, confidentiality and
  non-solicitation clauses before taking clients. Keep the practice fully separate: no employer time, devices,
  email, data, templates or contacts. Jayvin appears as the preparer; nothing learned as an employee is used.

## 5. The AI-run system (what Claude runs vs what Ann does)
| Step | Who |
|---|---|
| Bank and card feeds into QuickBooks Online | Client connects once; Ann approves access |
| Transaction categorisation and matching | Claude (QuickBooks connector), Ann spot-checks |
| Reconciliations each month | Claude drafts, Ann signs off |
| Receipt and document chasing emails | Claude drafts, sent from Ann's practice email |
| Month-end report, board pack, variance notes, cash forecast | Claude drafts in Ann's house style; Ann reviews and sends |
| Budget builds, audit schedules, policy manuals | Claude drafts from templates; Ann edits |
| Client calls, finance committee meetings, sign-offs | Ann |
| Prospecting list, outreach drafts, follow-ups | Claude (jayvin-bizdev-pipeline); Ann approves each send |
| Invoicing and collections reminders | Claude drafts; Ann approves |

**QuickBooks + Claude:** yes. A QuickBooks connector is available for Claude (it can read bank-feed transactions with
QuickBooks' suggestions, propose a category, split, transfer or match for each, and post only after Ann says yes; it
can also run reconciliations and reports). It needs authorising once in claude.ai Settings > Connectors. With a
QuickBooks Online Accountant account, each client company is a separate file; check after connecting whether the
connector can switch between client companies or needs connecting per client.

## 6. 90-day launch (start whenever Ann says go)
1. **Week 1-2:** pick the name and domain, register the trade name, engagement letter and AI-use clause, E&O quote,
   QuickBooks Online Accountant (free) and connector set up, practice email on the new domain.
2. **Week 3-4:** website live at the new domain (draft ready, see below); LinkedIn profile line updated to the
   practice only if Ann is comfortable; a one-page PDF of the offer.
3. **Month 2:** 2 pilot clients at a launch rate (for example Board Ready at CA$1,150 for 3 months) in exchange for a
   written testimonial and a referral if they are happy. Sources: Ann's own network outside her employer,
   nonprofit sector job boards and newsletters, local nonprofit networks.
4. **Month 3:** first 3 to 4 paying clients; tighten the monthly routine so Claude does the first draft of everything.
5. **Months 4-12:** grow to 6 to 10 clients mostly by referral; raise prices for new clients once the calendar is
   two-thirds full.
