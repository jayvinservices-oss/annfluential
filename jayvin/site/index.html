What a month looks like

A close you can set your calendar by

Working days after month end, on QuickBooks Online.

Day 1 to 3

Bank and card transactions categorised and matched. Missing receipts requested in one email, not ten.

Day 4 to 5

Every bank, card and loan account reconciled to its statement. Payables and receivables checked.

Day 6 to 8

Month closed. Budget against actual, with a plain-language note on anything that moved more than you expected.

Day 9 to 10

Board or owner pack sent: results, cash on hand, a three-month cash forecast and restricted funds.

How the work gets done

Software does the sorting. A finance director signs it off.

Routine steps like categorising transactions and drafting reconciliations are prepared with AI tools inside QuickBooks Online. Nothing is posted or sent until it has been reviewed by an internationally qualified Chartered Accountant.

Your data stays in your QuickBooks file. It is not used to train AI models, and every report you receive has been read by a person before it reaches you.
