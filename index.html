How this system works

People write the Sheet, Claude writes everything else. Ann and Augusta update the Status column in the action tracker Sheet; Claude reads it but never edits it. Tracker rules: items are sorted by priority (CRITICAL and NOW on top); anything marked done disappears from this view; Claude may add new items or re-prioritize, but an item is only ever closed when Ann explicitly says it's done. Routing: new tasks default to Augusta or AI, never auto-assigned to Ann. Augusta's daily work briefs: she works Tue–Sat; the Tuesday 4 AM run fills her brief for Tue, Wed and Thu, and the Friday 4 AM run fills Fri and Sat plus the weekend plan — each brief = the standing basics (out-folder review, schedule at peak times, publish trials from the IG app, engage) plus one or two specific priority items, so the week's open work is split across her days. Changes Ann makes over the weekend get picked up Tuesday. Ann's open CRITICAL/NOW items ride in the 9 AM refresh event. Every Tuesday and Friday at 4 AM Toronto (9 AM Lagos) a scheduled run pulls Metricool + the Sheet (including anything added manually), rebuilds this page, refreshes best times and competitor numbers, and updates the calendar briefs.
