# Cat purring videos: is it worth it? (YouTube scan, 5 Nov 2026)

211 long videos from 13 searches (cat purring, petting cat ASMR, sleeping with cat, AI cat). Data:
metadata/competitors/cats-2026-11-05.yaml.

## What works
- **Very long purring for sleep earns huge watch hours.** Median length of the top 15 purring videos: 10 hours.
  "Cozy Purring Cat ASMR | Peaceful Evening Fireplace and Gentle Purr" (24 h, 3.1M views, 49x its subscribers);
  "Happy Cat Purring Smoothly - Comforting Sounds for Sleep" (3 h, 9M); "1 Hour | ASMR: Cats Purring for
  Relaxation and Deep Sleep" (3.1M, 69x subscribers).
- **A person + their cat, for sleep, is a proven format right now.** Tabu Tunes: "ASMR Spiritual Sleep Clinic
  with My Cat (Purring)" 105 min, 6,400 views a day; "ASMR ... with My Cat" 93 min, 7,400 a day (8 to 11x
  subscribers). "giving my cat (and u) tingles (asmr)" 1.5M. Cat massage / petting ASMR 1 hour: 2 to 3.8k a day.
- **Title words that win:** sleep, purring, cozy, rain, fireplace, deep, ambience, hours, "with my cat".
- **Pairing purring with rain or fireplace** is in most of the top 20.

## Fit for Annie ASMR
- Strong. Real purring from Ace (11 minutes recorded, cleaned, no talking) loops well for 1 to 8 hours.
- Cheapest video we can make: a sleeping scene changes very little, so 3 to 4 short clips loop for hours
  (about $0.50 in generation for an 8-hour video). Watch hours per view are the highest of any format.
- Risk: YouTube can treat a long single-scene loop as "repetitive content". Keep it varied: 3 to 4 scenes,
  slow camera changes, chapters (petting, settling, sleeping, rain), real audio, and only a few of these a month.
