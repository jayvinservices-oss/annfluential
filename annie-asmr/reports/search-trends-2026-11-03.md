## Title wording tips [AC frequency]
- "no talking" is the most common modifier: it appears under almost every seed. Next come "sleep"/"for sleep", "mouth sounds", "tapping and scratching", hour counts (1/3/8/10 hours), "no ads", "eyes closed", "tingles", "lofi", "aggressive"/"fast".
- Start titles with "ASMR" followed by the trigger, as searchers type them ("asmr gum chewing", not "chewing gum asmr"). For holiday videos, "christmas asmr ..." also works.
- Combine two triggers with "and": autocomplete contains "nails and mouth sounds", "tapping and mouth sounds" and "gum chewing and tapping".
- Avoid the roleplay, girlfriend and kisses phrasing, which is popular but against the brand rules.
