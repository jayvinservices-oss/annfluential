# What the winning ASMR videos do (YouTube scan, 4 Nov 2026)

Source: 403 long-form videos (3+ min) from 22 searches across our niches (gum, knuckles, lip gloss, nails, Christmas,
crafts, sleep, AI ASMR). Public numbers only: views, likes, length, age, title, chapters, thumbnail. Other channels'
watch time is private; "watch hours" below is estimated as views x length x 25%. Full data:
metadata/competitors/2026-11-04.yaml (python -m tools.competitor_scan to refresh).

## The 8 patterns
1. **Long sleep videos earn the watch hours.** The top earners run 1 to 3 hours; sleep listeners leave them playing.
   Median length of the top 15: sleep 195 min, knuckles/cracking 78, nails 63, Christmas 59, lip gloss 38, gum 34.
2. **"No Talking" is the strongest title phrase** (35 of the top 120 titles). Viewers search it.
3. **Chapters** appear on most top tapping and sleep videos.
4. **"Cracking" now means wax, clay and nail polish cracking.** It is the fastest-growing trigger in the scan
   (Clay Butter ASMR 81,600 views a day; Satisfying Wax Cracking 53,000 a day, 120x the channel's subscribers;
   Nail Polish Cracking 1 to 10 Layers 19,000 a day). Knuckle cracking itself is a small niche.
5. **Lip gloss layering works long.** "1 HOUR, 100 LAYERS ~ Ear to Ear Lipgloss Tingles" 4.1M views;
   "wet and sticky lipgloss mouth sounds (No Talking)" 4x the channel's subscribers.
6. **Long nail tapping with "Addictive"/"Fast"** is steady and fresh (tabi ASMR, 63 min, 7,550 views a day).
7. **Christmas ASMR is thin in search**; the strong Christmas-time videos are cozy pampering (Latte ASMR, 60 min,
   913k) and unboxing ("65 minutes of mystery bags ASMR NO TALKING", 4.1M). Gifts = unboxing language.
8. **AI ASMR that wins is honest and object-led**: AI glass cutting 12 hours (666k), AI fruit cutting shorts.
   "Not AI. Just Real" titles show some viewers push back, so the short disclosure stays.

## Thumbnails (12 winners checked)
- Close crop, one subject filling the frame: lips + gloss wand, or nails on the object.
- Two to three big words at most ("ADDICTIVE TAPPING", "HUGE BUBBLE", "No Talking" pill) or none.
- Bright, soft colour; pink and nude dominate in the gloss and gum niches.
