# Weekly scheduled stream: plan (4 Nov 2026)

Approved by Ann ("weekly 2-hour scheduled live stream"). Since only new AI content is published, the stream is a
**YouTube Premiere**: a finished 2-hour video that plays at a set time with live chat, then stays public as a normal
video. Its watch time counts toward the 4,000 hours, like any public video. No 24/7 loops, never unscheduled.

## Slot
- **Fridays, 9:00 pm Toronto** (late evening for North America, sleep time; weekend for viewers in the UK early on Saturday).
- Announce it **one week ahead**: the Premiere page goes up the Friday before (a waiting page collects reminders).
- First one: **Friday 4 December 2026**, after the first Christmas video (1 December).

## Format: "Friday Night Sleep Session" (2 hours)
- 0:00 brand whisper, then straight into the trigger (no intro talk).
- Four 30-minute blocks, each a different trigger, slowed and dim: gum and crackling, lip gloss and nail taps, a
  Christmas craft (cards, ornaments or wrapping), slow gum pops and goodnight whispers.
- Made **mostly from new footage**; at most one block may reuse a recent video's footage, re-cut. YouTube's review
  rejects channels that mostly repeat the same footage.
- Title pattern: `ASMR for Sleep 2 Hours | Gum, Gloss & Nail Tapping | Friday Night Sleep Session | AI ASMR`
- Chapters for each block; playlist: Long ASMR for Sleep.

## In the chat (Ann, optional, 10 minutes at the start)
- A pinned comment: "Tell me which sound you want next Friday." The answers feed the next session.
- Nothing personal, no pet names; requests are noted, never promised.

## What Claude does
- Assembles the 2-hour video from the storyboard blocks once clips and sounds are in.
- Uploads it as a private draft with title, description, chapters and thumbnail; Ann sets it to Premiere in Studio
  (YouTube does not let the uploader app schedule a Premiere, and uploads stay private until Ann says yes).
