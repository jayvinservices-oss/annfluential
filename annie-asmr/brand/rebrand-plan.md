# Annie ASMR rebrand plan (3 Nov 2026)

Goal: the channel looks like one thing (glossy, slow, up-close ASMR for sleep) before the new videos
and the Christmas run start on 1 December, so new viewers who click a video find more like it.
Each step waits for Ann's yes on the production board. The style guide is `brand/style-guide.md`.

## Week 1 (by Sun 8 Nov): the channel page
1. Ann approves the style guide and the artwork drafts.
2. Ann gives Claude access to the channel (board item "Give Claude access to the channel").
3. Claude, after Ann's yes: banner, About text and keywords, the six series playlists, old videos sorted into them.
4. Ann in Studio: profile picture, handle, channel trailer and home-page sections (YouTube does not let apps change these).

## Week 2 (by Sun 15 Nov): the old videos that earn the hours
5. Every old video gets a PG-13 title with no teasing and a new-style thumbnail (drafts below and in
   brand/retitles.yaml; final wording after the cut-by-cut analysis). Keep the words people search for
   ("gum chewing", "popping", "crackling"); fix typos ("aand"). Ann approves the list.
   Order: the small videos first, then the gum videos; the top gum video's thumbnail first and its title
   last, watching the click rate, because it brings three quarters of the hours.
6. Keep every old video public until the Partner Program is reached (private videos stop counting toward
   the 4,000 hours). The mouth-sound and singing videos stay out of playlists and off the home page.

## From week 2: new videos in the new style
7. First new video: Fast Gum Popping & Knuckle Cracking (a follow-up to the best performer).
8. Christmas videos made by 24 Nov, published from 1 Dec, each in its series.
9. Each long video gets one Short trailer pointing to it.

## Monetisation
See brand/monetisation-plan.md: longer videos, more gum, scheduled live streams, click rate.

## Measures (weekly, on the board)
Click rate (now 4.8%), average view (now 4:31), watch hours per day (now 1.9), subscribers.
