# Monetisation by January: the honest numbers and everything we can do (3 Nov 2026)

## Where the channel is
- Subscribers 1,034 of 1,000: met.
- Public watch hours, last 365 days: 543 of 4,000. Gap 3,457 hours.
- Last 28 days: 51.9 hours (1.9 a day). To reach 4,000 by 17 Jan: about 46 hours a day, 25 times today.
- The other route, 10 million Shorts views in 90 days, is further away still.
- A lower tier exists (500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days, 3,000 hours or 3 million Shorts views). It
  opens channel memberships and Super Thanks but not ad revenue, and needs 2,457 more hours.

**Plainly: 17 January needs a big jump, not a steady climb.** The plan below goes for the levers that
can move hours fastest. If January slips, everything still counts: the 365-day window keeps rolling.

## The levers, biggest first
1. **Longer videos.** The two 30-minute gum videos earn almost all the hours; the 4 to 10 minute ones
   earn almost none. Every new video 45 to 90 minutes, sleep-friendly (no sudden loud parts, dim light,
   slow end). Sleep viewers leave videos playing, so one 1-hour video watched for 25 minutes by
   400 people is 166 hours.
2. **More gum.** Gum Club weekly. It is what the channel is found for. The "with a friend" format is the
   number one and number two video: a new real gum video with the same friend is the single best bet.
3. **Scheduled live streams, archived public.** Archived live streams count toward the hours. A weekly
   2-hour "gum and nails, study with me" stream, announced a week ahead. Only scheduled streams, no 24/7 loops.
4. **Click rate.** 4.8% now. New-style thumbnails and clean titles aim for 6 to 8%, which means more
   viewers from the same impressions.
5. **The first 30 seconds.** Half of viewers left the latest video by 0:05. Trigger first, no intro,
   in every new video (already built into the pipeline).
6. **Keep viewers on the channel.** Series playlists, end screens to the next video, cards, and a channel
   trailer for new visitors.
7. **Shorts as trailers.** A Short for every long video, linked to it. Shorts views do not count as hours,
   but they bring people to the long videos that do.
8. **Christmas.** Seasonal searches peak in December. Four Christmas videos ready by 24 November.
9. **Community posts.** A poll each week ("which sound next?"); it reminds subscribers and feeds requests.

## What YouTube checks when you apply
YouTube reviews the channel by hand. Channels of mass-produced or repetitive AI videos are refused
("inauthentic content"). To pass:
- every video different (theme, objects, sounds); the pipeline's variety check enforces it;
- real recorded sounds; Ann's own voice clone and real Ann videos in the mix;
- disclosure in every AI video, and titles that match the content;
- the old videos retitled to PG-13 so nothing reads as sexual content to the reviewer.
