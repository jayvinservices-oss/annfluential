# Books and Other Low-Effort Products for a Real-Cat Brand (Ace, Toronto), 2025-2026

Research date: 5 November 2026. Method note: the session's network blocked direct fetches of most primary pages (kdp.amazon.com, authorsguild.org, support.patreon.com, kindlepreneur.com, neolemon.com, vappingo.com, inkfluenceai.com). Findings below come from search-engine extracts of those pages plus secondary sources. Figures attributed to a KDP help page come from the search extract of that page, not a full read. Every number should be checked on the primary page before pricing decisions. Many "earnings" sources in this niche are tool vendors or course sellers; they are flagged as such.

## KDP and other book routes for cat content (rules, costs, royalties, limits, realistic sales, alternatives)

### Takeaway
KDP allows AI-generated text and images but requires a private per-title disclosure of AI-generated (not AI-assisted) content and caps uploads at 3 titles per day. The June 2025 change lowered paperback royalties on books under US$9.99 to 50%. A 32-page square colour picture book is feasible on KDP with premium colour, at a printing cost of about US$4.20 or C$5.26. Realistic sales for a new self-published picture book or low-content book run from a handful of copies a year to a few hundred. Income needs a catalogue, an audience, or both. A true "purring" sound book cannot be made through print-on-demand. It needs a factory run of roughly 500 to 3,000 or more units.

### Cited Findings

**AI rules and publishing limits**
- On 18 September 2023, Amazon announced on the KDP forum that it would limit self-publishers to three new titles per day, citing AI concerns. Amazon said the number could be adjusted "if needed". Anyone who hits the limit is notified. — [Ebook Friendly](https://ebookfriendly.com/amazon-kdp-publishers-publish-more-than-3-books-a-day/); [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/?p=228248). (Dated 2023. No evidence was found of a 2025-2026 change to the cap.)
- AI disclosure is a private checkbox between the publisher and Amazon, answered for each title in the KDP publishing form. It is not shown to buyers. — [ScribeCount KDP AI Content Policy guide](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide)
- The disclosure is made per title and per format. A book with AI-generated content in both ebook and paperback is disclosed separately for each. — [ScribeCount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/KDP%20AI%20Content%20Policy%20)
- KDP distinguishes "AI-generated" content (text, images or translations created by AI), which must be disclosed, from "AI-assisted" content (human-created, then edited or brainstormed with AI), which need not be. Secondary guides describe the dividing line as "substantial modification". — [builtwritten.com](https://www.builtwritten.com/blog/can-you-publish-an-ai-written-book-on-kdp); [ScribeCount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide). (Primary KDP Content Guidelines page G200672390 could not be fetched.)
- Some blogs pass on unverified pacing tips, such as publishing only 2 a day. These have no primary support. — per search summary of [bookwriter.vip 2026 guide](https://www.bookwriter.vip/amazon-kdp-ai-publishing-guide-2026)

**Royalties (paperback and ebook)**
- From 10 June 2025, KDP cut the paperback royalty rate from 60% to 50% for books priced below certain list prices, for example under US$9.99. Books at or above the threshold stay at 60% minus printing cost. — [KDP Help GXFPA52P6ZJD2U3N](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GXFPA52P6ZJD2U3N); [Bublish](https://blog.bublish.com/royalty-changes-at-amazons-kindle-direct-publishing-kdp); [writem substack](https://writem.substack.com/p/what-the-new-amazon-print-royalty)
- Royalty = list price (ex-tax) x royalty rate - printing cost. To earn the 60% tier, the minimum price is printing cost / 0.60. — [kdpbuilder worked examples, revised 2026-09-09](https://kdpbuilder.com/resources/kdp-print-pricing-worked-examples)
- In the same June 2025 change, Amazon lowered colour print costs in some marketplaces. US standard colour per-page cost on regular trim fell from $0.0270 to $0.0255. Premium colour cuts were mainly in the UK and EU, and US premium colour was unchanged. — [KDP Help GXFPA52P6ZJD2U3N](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GXFPA52P6ZJD2U3N)

**Print costs for colour picture books**
- Premium colour paperbacks start at 24 pages. Standard colour (the cheaper option) starts at 72 pages, so a 32-page picture book must use premium colour on KDP. — [KDP Help G201834340](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340); [inkfluenceai IngramSpark calculator](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/ingramspark-cost-calculator)
- Premium colour with 24-40 pages is a flat fee with no per-page charge. Amazon.com charges US$3.60 for regular trim and US$4.20 for large trim. Amazon.ca charges C$4.66 for regular trim and C$5.26 for large trim. 8.5x8.5 counts as large trim; the large-trim column is inferred from the table layout. — [KDP Help G201834340](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340)
- Above 40 pages, Amazon.com large-trim premium colour costs about US$0.08 per page. — same [KDP Help page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340), per search extract
- Standard colour can almost halve colour printing cost compared with premium colour, but only from 72 pages. That suits a longer cat photo book, not a 32-page picture book. — [Self-Publishing substack](https://selfpublishing.substack.com/p/amazon-adds-cut-price-color-printing-choice-for-kdp-print-books)
- Printing cost depends on the marketplace, page count and ink type. Trim, bleed and cover finish do not affect it. — [KDP Help GXFPA52P6ZJD2U3N](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GXFPA52P6ZJD2U3N)

**Low-content books (journals, coloring books, photo books)**
- Generic low-content books are saturated. Amazon shoppers see "thousands of nearly identical results" for terms like "Gratitude Journal", and basic journals and notebooks are "extremely saturated". Specific sub-niche planners still sell. — [Naomi Jane substack](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon); [The Write Moves](https://the-write-moves.beehiiv.com/p/low-content-burnout-are-publishers-quitting-kdp-in-droves)
- Amazon does not classify coloring and puzzle books as low-content. Coloring books are a crowded market of over 100,000 active titles, and sales are seasonal, peaking at Christmas, New Year and back-to-school. — search summaries of [Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com/low-content-books/), [The Write Moves (coloring comeback)](https://the-write-moves.beehiiv.com/p/the-kdp-coloring-book-comeback-nostalgia-or-a-new-cash-cow-for-2025), [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12351)
- One 2026 guide suggests "two copies a book a month" as a fair starting assumption for a journal or coloring book catalogue, and says this is probably not a cautious figure. — [inkfluenceai 2026](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/low-content-book-income-2026) (vendor blog)

**Picture books about a cat: realistic sales**
- Amazon publishes no KDP sales benchmarks. Blogs agree most KDP authors make little. First-year earnings run "anywhere from a few dollars to a few hundred". The typical self-published book sells under 200 to about 300 copies in its lifetime. — [neolemon](https://www.neolemon.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-selling-childrens-books-on-kdp/); [Vappingo first KDP book](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=12349); [Goodreads author blog](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25972961)
- First-time picture-book authors often spend US$500-2,000 producing a quality book, and being in the red for the first year or two is common. — [neolemon](https://www.neolemon.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-selling-childrens-books-on-kdp/)
- Authors Guild 2023 survey (via secondary source): median income for full-time self-published authors was about US$12,800 a year from book sales. This covers all genres, is not specific to picture books, and is dated 2023. — [neolemon](https://www.neolemon.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-selling-childrens-books-on-amazon-kdp)
- Anecdote: a UK creator who published in January 2026 reported 7 copies sold. — [digitalrevenuestudios blog](https://digitalrevenuestudios.blogspot.com/2026/03/is-amazon-kdp-worth-it-in-uk-in-2026.html)

**Cat book examples**
- Successful cat photo books and calendars come mostly from traditional publishers working with large accounts. Andrew Marttila's "Cats on Catnip" (Workman) now runs as an annual wall calendar, with the 2027 edition at US$16.99. @cats_of_instagram became a Chronicle Books book of 400+ photos plus a daily calendar. — [Hachette Book Group](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/andrew-marttila-2/cats-on-catnip-wall-calendar-2027/9781523534838/); [Chronicle Books](https://www.chroniclebooks.com/products/cats-on-instagram)
- Comparable: the dog account Doggos Doing Things got a Running Press (Hachette) deal for a US$17 hardcover. — [Tubefilter, 2020](https://www.tubefilter.com/2020/03/19/doggos-doing-things-lands-hachette-book-deal/) (older)

**"Purring" novelty sound book**
- Custom sound modules and sound books are factory-made. Alibaba and Made-in-China listings show minimum orders of about 500-1,000 pieces at the low end, 3,000 commonly, and 5,000 for fully custom branding. Module unit prices run about US$0.30-4 depending on volume. Toy-safety certifications such as EN 71 and CE come up for children's products. — [Alibaba custom membrane module](https://www.alibaba.com/product-introduction/Custom-Pre-recorded-Membrane-Sound-Module_62283376984.html); [Alibaba sound board book](https://www.alibaba.com/product-introduction/Custom-Story-Sound-Board-Children-Book_1600717920009.html); [Made-in-China custom sound module](https://m.made-in-china.com/hot-china-products/Custom_Sound_Module-2.html) (marketplace listings, not firm quotes)

**Alternatives: IngramSpark and Lulu**
- IngramSpark changed its print-on-demand pricing on 1 April 2025; the new figures were not retrieved. — [IngramSpark 2025 price change](https://www.ingramspark.com/2025-price-change)
- Sources conflict on IngramSpark setup fees: one says account creation and uploads are free, while others cite US$49 a title. A 32-page 8.5x8.5 premium-colour picture book costs about US$4.20-4.44 per author copy at IngramSpark. IngramSpark is the route for dust-jacket hardcovers and wide distribution to bookstores and libraries. — [inkfluenceai IngramSpark calculator 2026](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/ingramspark-cost-calculator); [books.by comparison](https://books.by/compare/ingramspark-alternatives)
- Lulu has no ISBN or monthly fees. When selling through Lulu Direct (your own store), authors keep 100% of revenue after printing and shipping, but Lulu has much less traffic than Amazon. — [Lulu blog](https://blog.lulu.com/print-on-demand-costs-for-authors/); [Written Word Media](https://writtenwordmedia.com/lulu-book-publishing)

### Inferences
- Worked example (my calculation from the KDP figures above): a 32-page 8.5x8.5 premium-colour paperback at US$14.99 on Amazon.com earns about 14.99 x 0.60 - 4.20 = US$4.79 a copy. At US$9.99 it earns about 9.99 x 0.60 - 4.20 = US$1.79. Below US$9.99 the 50% rate applies and royalties shrink further. On Amazon.ca, with a C$5.26 print cost, a C$19.99 list price would give about C$6.73, assuming the 60% tier applies at that price (the CAD threshold was not confirmed).
- For Ace, a photo-based book using real photos of a real cat avoids most AI-disclosure exposure. A picture book illustrated with AI images would need the disclosure box ticked but is allowed. The real-cat angle is a differentiator against the flood of AI-made generic cat books.
- Generic cat journals and coloring books are poor bets. An "Ace" coloring book could work as fan merch sold to his own audience, but not as a way to be found by strangers on Amazon.
- KDP standard colour (72+ pages) makes a cheaper "year in the life of Ace" photo book viable.
- A purring sound book is the opposite of low-effort: it needs upfront inventory of roughly US$1,000-10,000+, safety certification and fulfilment. Cheaper substitutes are a QR code in a printed book linking to a purr audio track, or a digital purr track.
- Sequencing: ebook and paperback on KDP for both .com and .ca first, then IngramSpark for a hardcover once there is demand.

## Other emerging low-effort income ideas for a pet brand (status in 2026, costs, fees, earnings evidence, saturation)

### Takeaway
The clearest low-cost, fast options for a cat with an audience are fan-funding (Ko-fi, Patreon), a paid newsletter, and digital or print-on-demand merch built around Ace's own face. Generic AI digital products such as Canva templates, generic stickers, AI pet portraits and planners are heavily saturated. Etsy now requires AI disclosure. NFTs should be avoided. A sleep or purr app can earn in principle, but it is a crowded, effortful software business. Plush licensing through Makeship-style campaigns is plausible later, once Ace has a fan base.

### Cited Findings

**Patreon, Ko-fi and Substack**
- Patreon applies a single standard platform fee to creators who publish a page after 4 August 2025: 10%, plus payment processing. Legacy creators keep their old 8% or 12% plans. — [Patreon Help Center](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/36426991446797); [Bloomberg, 16 June 2025](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-16/patreon-raises-fees-for-new-creators-to-support-video-content); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/6/patreon-is-increasing-platform-fees-for-new-creators-starting-august-2025/)
- Ko-fi takes 0% on one-off tips and crowdfunding goals. On the free plan, memberships, monthly tips, commissions and shop sales carry 5%. Ko-fi Gold (about US$6 a month) is reported as 0% platform fee. Sources describe Gold differently, so verify on ko-fi.com. — [cartmango Patreon vs Ko-fi](https://cartmango.com/patreon-vs-ko-fi/); [schoolmaker](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/free-patreon-alternatives)
- Substack charges no upfront fee and takes 10% of paid subscriptions, plus Stripe's 2.9% + 30c and a billing fee of 0.5-0.7%. The effective total is about 13-16%. Most subscriptions are priced at US$5-15 a month. — [Amy Suto](https://www.amysuto.com/desk-of-amy-suto/substack-pricing); [schoolmaker Substack review 2026](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/substack-review.md)

**Etsy rules and AI digital products**
- Etsy's Creativity Standards (July 2024) place AI-generated items under "Designed by a seller", not "Made by a seller". AI use must be disclosed in the listing description. — [Entrepreneur](https://entrepreneur.com/business-news/is-ai-allowed-on-etsy-new-standards-clarify-whats-allowed/476821); [listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards)
- In mid-2025, Etsy reportedly removed "or using a templated design or pattern" from its standards, so items must be "based on a seller's original design". Enforcement runs from listing removal up to shop suspension. — [listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards); [Digital Commerce 360, Oct 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/26/etsy-ai-policy/amp/) (the primary Etsy page was not read)
- Canva templates are saturated. Packs that sold for US$27-47 in 2024 now compete with near-identical packs at US$5-9. Etsy has raised seller fees three times since 2023. — [passive-income-tools blog](https://passive-income-tools.pages.dev/blog/selling-canva-templates-passive-income-2026) (low-authority blog)
- Digital sticker packs sell for about US$3-8 (mega packs US$10-20). Sources disagree on whether competition is high or medium. Phone wallpapers have no solid data. One promotional example assumes US$1.78 a sale at one sale a day, about US$53 a month. — [insightagent guide](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/best-digital-downloads-etsy); [marketingwords](https://www.marketingwords.com/products/make-wallpaper-for-phone/)

**AI pet portraits as a service**
- Etsy's pet-portrait market is saturated, though quality, turnaround and presentation still win. Professional pet portraits typically sell for US$50-300. — [findniche Etsy listing analysis](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/custom-watercolor-paint-portraits-using--1582219017); [artomate pets niche guide](https://www.artomate.app/niche-guides/complete-guide-to-selling-ai-art-in-the-pets-animals-niche)
- Searches for "pet portrait custom" rose from about 4,400 in May 2025 to a peak of 14,800 in December 2025. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/pet-portrait-custom)
- Claims such as "US$2,000-10,000 a month" for top AI art sellers come from tool vendors and are unverified. — [zsky.ai](https://zsky.ai/blog/ai-generated-art-that-sold.html); [ventureaiart](https://ventureaiart.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-make-500-week-selling-custom-ai-pet-portraits)

**Calendars**
- The cat wall-calendar market is served by traditional publishers at about US$16.99, for example Workman's "Cats on Catnip". — [Hachette Book Group](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/andrew-marttila-2/cats-on-catnip-wall-calendar-2027/9781523534838/)

**Purring or sleep app**
- Sleep-sound apps can earn well at the top. Bedtime Fan reportedly makes about US$50K a month, through App Store keyword targeting and niche variants such as green and brown noise. White Noise Deep Sleep Sounds is estimated at about US$150K a month. Most small apps estimate under US$10K a month, and the category is crowded with dozens of near-identical apps. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-simple-sleep-sound-app-makes-50k-month-SGIFPK5GRzQEnh1TYs0W); [screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/white-noise-deep-sleep-sounds); [AppGoblin listings](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6443706937) (third-party estimates)

**Plush and character licensing**
- Makeship is a Canadian company that makes limited-edition plush with small and mid-sized online creators. Campaigns must hit a funding goal before production, which lowers financial risk, and Makeship handles manufacturing. The revenue split was not found. — [Wikipedia: Makeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makeship); [LaunchBoom review](https://www.launchboom.com/blog/makeship-review/)
- Influencers "large and small" are increasingly built into licensing strategies. Spin Master's P.Lushes Pets gave six social influencers their own plush characters. — [License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/entertainment/social-media-influencers-collaborate-spin-master-plushes-pets-plush)

**NFTs**
- NFT sales were US$320.2M in November 2025, down from US$629M in October (CryptoSlam). Annual 2025 volume was about US$5.5B, down about 37% year over year. Marketplaces have closed (X2Y2, Nifty Gateway), Blur is winding down, and NFT Paris 2026 was cancelled over the downturn. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/features/what-the-nft-paris-cancellation-says-about-the-current-state-of-the-nft-market); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/380802?amp=1); [blockeden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2025/08/20/nifty-gateway-shutdown-nft-market-collapse/)

### Inferences

**Best effort-to-income and fastest first dollar**
1. **Ko-fi tip jar or shop, set up today.** 0% fees on tips, digital downloads of real Ace photos, and a "buy Ace a treat" button. Revenue scales with audience size.
2. **Real-photo Ace digital products and print-on-demand merch** (wallpapers, sticker packs, calendar). The "original design" requirement favours real photos of a real cat over generic AI art, and they are Ace-branded, not generic.
3. **A cheap Patreon, Ko-fi or Substack membership** (US$3-5) for behind-the-scenes content once there is a core audience. Expect 10-16% in fees.
4. **A KDP photo book or picture book** as a slower, catalogue-style earner sold to the fan base, available on both .com and .ca.

**Mid-term, if audience grows**
- A Makeship-style plush campaign. It carries no inventory risk, Makeship is a Canadian company, and it suits a distinctive tuxedo cat.
- Pitching a trad photo book or calendar deal. The Marttila and @cats_of_instagram precedents show publishers buy cat content from big accounts.

**Avoid in 2026**
- NFTs.
- Generic Canva templates, AI planners and generic low-content journals, which are saturated and low-priced.
- An AI pet portraits service, which is saturated and unrelated to Ace's brand; it competes on labour, not on Ace.
- A purr app, unless Ann wants a software project. It is feasible, but it means a crowded App Store, app review and ongoing maintenance.
- A custom purring sound book. It needs factory minimums.

**Canada**
- Patreon, Ko-fi, Substack and Etsy all pay Canadian creators. Fees above are in USD. Currency and GST/HST registration (over C$30K) are outside this scope.
