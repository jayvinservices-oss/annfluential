# Selling and distributing real cat purring audio and sleep sounds (2025-2026)

Research date: 5 Nov 2026. Creator is in Toronto, Canada. Method note: in this session, artists.spotify.com, support.google.com (YouTube Help) and musicbusinessworldwide.com could not be fetched because the session's network settings blocked them. Findings rely on search-indexed snippets of those and other pages. Primary-source confirmation is flagged where missing. Most Spotify rule details date from the Nov 2023 announcement and the April 2024 rollout, so they are older than 2025. I found no evidence that they have been reversed.

## 1. Streaming (Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon) via distributors

### Takeaway
Spotify deliberately cut what noise tracks earn in 2024. Noise counts as a royalty-bearing stream only at 2+ minutes and is paid at a fraction of the music rate (reportedly about 20%). Each track also needs 1,000 streams in 12 months plus an undisclosed minimum of unique listeners before it earns anything. Distributors and stores are increasingly rejecting pure noise and field recordings, so a raw purring album has both a low ceiling and a real risk of rejection.

### Cited Findings
- From 2024, Spotify requires "functional noise" recordings to be at least 2 minutes long to earn royalties. Covered categories: white noise, nature sounds, machine noises, sound effects, non-spoken ASMR and silence. The stated reason was "bad actors" cutting noise into 30-second tracks and stacking them in playlists. Two minutes of noise listening now generates 1 royalty-bearing stream instead of 4. — [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2023/11/22/tech/spotify-slashes-royalties-functional-noise); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/11/23/spotify-cuts-royalties-rain-sounds-white-noise-asmr)
- Spotify said it would "value noise streams at a fraction of the value of music streams." Billboard reported, and Radio X repeated, that functional tracks are worth about one-fifth (20%) of music streams. Spotify did not state the figure itself. — [KSL/CNN wire](https://www.ksl.com/article/50797802/spotify-to-slash-royalties-for-rain-sounds-white-noise-and-other-non-music-tracks); [Radio X](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2023/11/21/spotify-plans-to-pay-80-less-for-rain-sounds-and-white-noise/)
- TuneCore's help centre confirms that eligible functional noise recordings over 2 minutes "will be subject to reduced royalty rates" per Spotify policy. — [TuneCore support](https://support.tunecore.com/hc/en-au/articles/115006695068)
- Since 1 April 2024, a track needs 1,000 streams in the previous 12 months to earn recorded royalties. It also needs a minimum number of unique listeners, which Spotify does not disclose. Spotify says 99.5% of streams are on tracks above the threshold. — [Music Business Worldwide](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/changes-to-spotifys-royalty-model-including-the-1000-annual-streams-royalty-policy-are-officially-live); [MBW explainer](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/heres-exactly-spotifys-new-1000-annual-streams-royalty-policy-works123); [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2024/04/03/spotify-royalty-limit-for-streams/)
- Artificial streaming: Spotify charges labels and distributors per track when it detects flagrant artificial streaming. Press reports put the fee at about $10 per track. Spotify itself did not publish that figure. — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/spotify-confirm-changes-to-streaming-royalties/); [Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/?p=79343)
- Spotify also removed white-noise podcasts from its Ambassador Ads programme. Bloomberg estimated this added $38M a year to Spotify's gross profit. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2023/11/23/spotify-cuts-royalties-rain-sounds-white-noise-asmr)
- Context: Liz Pelly's Harper's piece (Jan 2025) alleged that Spotify fills mood and chill playlists with "ghost artists" (500+ aliases, about 20 writers) to lower royalty costs. That would make editorial playlists in this space harder to win. Spotify has denied similar claims before. — [Harper's](https://harpers.org:443/archive/2025/01/the-ghosts-in-the-machine-liz-pelly-spotify-musicians); [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/spotify-populating-playlists-ghost-artists-minimise-royalty-costs-report-alleges)
- Per-stream estimates (third-party, 2025): Spotify about $0.003-0.005 (blended about $0.0033, so about 300 streams per US$1). Apple Music about $0.007-0.01. Amazon about $0.004-0.0096, varying by tier. Spotify says there is no fixed per-stream rate. — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/statistics/what-streaming-platforms-actually-pay-per-stream); [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/01/24/apple-music-royalty-rate-spotify-study/); [ScreenApp](https://screenapp.io/blog/who-pays-artists-more-spotify-apple-music-tidal)
- Distributor acceptance:
  - Users report that DistroKid rejects "noises and field recordings," with some uploads passing and others refused. — [Spotify Community thread](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Content-Questions/Which-Distributor-for-uploading-Field-recordings-noises-etc/td-p/5223130)
  - RouteNote says iTunes/Apple and Gracenote won't accept white-noise-type releases. — [RouteNote support](https://support.routenote.com/?p=4912)
  - Other distributors list nature sounds and white noise as commonly rejected or not distributed: Sounds.co calls it "functional/generic music," and Interspace and AllOutLines list it as misleading or low-quality content. — [Sounds.co](https://support.sounds.co/en/articles/8411576-what-is-functional-or-generic-music-and-why-can-t-you-distribute-it); [Interspace](https://help.interspacemusic.com/article/unacceptable-misleading-tracks); [AllOutLines](https://allotlines.tawk.help/article/release-standards-and-content-quality)
  - Ambient uploads such as rain or city noise are the most exposed to "too similar to existing content" rejections and later takedowns. — [Whipped Cream Sounds](https://www.whippedcreamsounds.com/distrokid-wait-times)
- Example of existing purring catalogue: "Calming Sounds from TraxLab" has released dozens of "Cat Purring, Pt. 01-19+" tracks. On Last.fm (not Spotify) each track has only about 10-35 listeners. "Cat Music Experience" released "Cat Music Cuddles" (2025) and "Relax Cat" (2023). — [Last.fm TraxLab](https://www.last.fm/music/Calming+Sounds+from+TraxLab/+tracks); [Viberate](https://www.viberate.com/artist/cat-music-experience)
- Purring is also being sold as audiobooks on Audible and Audiobooks Now: "Cat Purring Therapy" and "Purring Cat & Soft Breath Sleep Sounds" (5 hours, by Sleeptime Publications). — [Audible.de](https://www.audible.de/pd/Cat-Purring-Therapy-Hoerbuch/B0H7RB52RM); [AudiobooksNow](https://www.audiobooksnow.com/audiobook/purring-cat-soft-breath-sleep-sounds/10462648/)

### Inferences
- Example maths at about $0.0033 per music stream and 20% for noise: about $0.00066 per noise stream, or roughly $0.66 per 1,000 streams. 100,000 streams a month would be about US$66 (CA$90). Purring could be classed as "nature sounds/sound effects," so assume the noise rate applies.
- Pure purring is likely to hit distributor rejection or the noise discount. Blending purring with original music (piano, ambient pads) may be treated as music, but that changes the product and still carries "functional/generic" rejection risk.
- Streaming is mainly worth it for discoverability (a "real cat" brand story), not for income.

### Gaps
- I could not open Spotify's official "Modernizing our royalty system" page or confirm any 2025-2026 changes to the noise valuation. I found no reporting on changes after 2024.
- Spotify monthly-listener counts for any cat-purring artist were not available from indexed sources. Check Spotify or Chartmetric directly.
- DistroKid, TuneCore and CD Baby have no formal written policy in the sources found. Amuse was not checked.

## 2. Sleep apps (Calm, BetterSleep, Endel, Noisli, Tide)

### Takeaway
I found no open creator-submission or revenue-share programme at any of the named sleep apps. Calm licenses catalogue mostly through label-style deals. Purring has also become its own app niche: several standalone "cat purr sleep" apps launched in 2025, which makes them competitors rather than buyers.

### Cited Findings
- Calm Music has signed label-style licensing for sleep and meditation music, reaching over 134M streams to date, and hired a UMG veteran. — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/Calm-App-Music-Heavily-into-Signing-Music-Licenses-for-Sleep-and-Meditation-Music-and-Hires-UMG-Veteran)
- In Oct 2025, Calm's announced licensing deals were product licensing (bedding and sleep tech), not sound sourcing. — [License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/health-wellness/calm-meditation-and-sleep-story-app-nabs-licensing-deals)
- BetterSleep advertises "more than 300 sleep sounds & music tracks." No external submission programme was found. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id314498713)
- A third-party guide says wellness apps (Calm, Headspace, Insight Timer) need large ambient catalogues. Typical delivery specs are 44.1/48 kHz, 16/24-bit WAV, roughly -20 to -14 LUFS. The source is a vendor guide, not app documentation. — [Orphiq](https://orphiq.com/resources/music-meditation-wellness-apps)
- Competing purr-specific iOS apps already exist: "Cat Purr - Sleep & Calm Sounds," "Purrly - Sleep in Purr" and "Meowment." — [Cat Purr app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6755916976); [Purrly](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6751825018); [Meowment](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6744857964)

### Inferences
- Selling to these apps would mean cold outreach or licensing through a catalogue or label, not a self-serve upload. Expect a one-off buyout, if anything.
- Insight Timer does accept independent creators. That may be the only open wellness-app route, but I did not verify it in this research.

### Gaps
- No submission or payment terms were found for Endel (generative, so it probably doesn't buy), Noisli, Tide or BetterSleep.

## 3. Stock audio and sound-effect marketplaces

### Takeaway
Individuals can sell cat-purr SFX on Pond5 at 30% and on AudioJungle/Envato. Adobe Stock does not accept audio from individuals. Epidemic Sound and Artlist work by curated buyout, not open upload. Supply is heavy and free purr SFX are abundant, so sales per clip are likely small.

### Cited Findings
- Pond5's contributor agreement, effective 15 Jan 2025, gives music and sound effects a 30% share of net license revenue. That is down from older 50% figures. Only exclusive video artists earn 40%. — [Pond5 Contributor Agreement](https://www.pond5.com/legal/contributor); [Pond5 Sell Your Media](https://www.pond5.com/sell-your-media); [StockPhotoSecrets](https://www.stockphotosecrets.com/?p=70674)
- Pond5 is owned by Shutterstock, which acquired it for about $210M (2022). — [RedShark News](https://www.redsharknews.com/shutterstock-acquires-pond5-for-210m?hs_amp=true)
- AudioJungle (Envato) lets authors choose exclusive or non-exclusive status. Non-exclusive authors pay a higher author fee. The figures of about 33% (non-exclusive) and 50-70% (exclusive) come from a low-quality source and are unverified. In 2024, Envato rolled back author-driven pricing for sound effects. — [Envato Author Help](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000471226); [Envato forums](https://forums.envato.com/t/author-driven-pricing-coming-to-audiojungle/182013/160)
- Adobe Stock: community moderators said in Sept 2025 that individual contributors cannot submit sound or music, AI or not. Adobe sources audio from partner agencies. — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/stock-contributors-discussions/does-adobe-stock-accept-ai-or-non-ai-sound-effects/td-p/15490398)
- Epidemic Sound pays upfront buyouts of USD 1,200-6,000 per music track (as of Q1 2022, said to have risen in 2023) plus a 50/50 streaming split. It works by curated artist deals, and no SFX submission portal was found. — [Epidemic Sustainability Report 2022](https://corporate.epidemicsound.com/files/Public/21928/3757291/sustainability-report-2022.pdf); [Epidemic artist FAQ](https://epidemicsound.com/campaign/artist-faq)
- Artlist's contributor terms are undisclosed or invite-only per third parties. — [Epidemic comparison](https://www.epidemicsound.com/blog/artlist-vs-epidemic-sound)
- Free purr SFX supply (a saturation signal):
  - ElevenLabs offers free cat-purring SFX downloads. — [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects/cat-purring)
  - Uppbeat hosts several purr SFX. — [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/sfx/cat-purr/14875/35338)
  - BigSoundBank offers a CC0 public-domain cat purr. — [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/cat-purr-s0436.html)

### Inferences
- Short purr clips compete with free and AI-generated purr SFX, so stock sales are likely to be a few dollars a month at most. Long, clean, high-resolution, well-tagged recordings (close-mic, different moods, kneading, sleepy purr) are the only differentiators.
- Pond5 non-exclusive leaves the creator free to sell elsewhere.

### Gaps
- No per-clip sales data for purring or animal SFX was found.
- Shutterstock's own audio contributor programme and BlackBox were not confirmed.
- Canadian tax forms (W-8BEN for US platforms) were not researched.

## 4. Digital downloads (Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip, Bandcamp)

### Takeaway
There is evidence of supply but no hard evidence of demand. Purr downloads exist on Gumroad (free up to £39) and as audiobooks. I found no reliable Etsy sales or listing counts for purring audio.

### Cited Findings
- On Gumroad, "getmishi" offers a free 20-minute cat-purr MP3 plus a looping MP4. The same seller sells a 37-minute "Deep Sleep" purr-and-tones soundscape for £39. — [Gumroad cat purrs](https://getmishi.gumroad.com/l/cat-purrs); [Gumroad deep sleep](https://getmishi.gumroad.com/l/deep-sleep-1am)
- Generic Etsy digital-download guides claim $100-1,000 a month. These figures are unsourced and are marketing content. — [Alura](https://www.alura.io/blog/ultimate-guide-to-selling-digital-downloads-on-etsy)

### Inferences
- Digital downloads work best as a brand or merch add-on: "sleep with [cat name]" bundles, a long purr MP3 plus phone wallpaper and a story about the cat, priced around CA$5-15. Selling them to an existing audience is more realistic than relying on marketplace search.

### Gaps
- Etsy listing counts and bestseller badges for "cat purring sound" could not be retrieved because search engines don't index Etsy listings well. Check Etsy directly.
- Payhip and Bandcamp were not checked for purring examples. Bandcamp's standard 15% digital revenue share was not verified in this session.

## 5. YouTube long-form sleep and purring content

### Takeaway
YouTube is the largest proven demand pool. Cat-relaxation channels have hundreds of millions of views, and sleep RPMs are roughly $2-6. The July 2025 "inauthentic content" rule applies to the whole channel and targets templated, near-identical videos, which is exactly what static-image 10-hour loops look like. Real, varied footage of one named cat is the main protection.

### Cited Findings
- From 15 July 2025, YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content," covering mass-produced and repetitive material. YouTube called it a "minor update to our longstanding guideline," with no new policy. Examples cited in coverage: videos with only slight variations, content made in bulk from the same template, and slideshows without narration. Enforcement applies to the whole channel. AI use is not banned. — [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-targets-mass-produced-content-in-monetization-update/550337/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155); [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660)
- One ambient-channel commentary argues that looping visuals and static images across near-identical uploads are the risk, and that the policy makes no genre exception. This is a marketing blog's interpretation, not YouTube's. No documented demonetisations of rain or white-noise channels were found. — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/sv/learn/youtube-music-promotion/ambient-music-channel-monetization-rules); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/faceless-youtube-channel-demonetized/)
- Sleep-sound RPM is roughly $2-6, per vendor guides. Estimator data for SleepDroid Studios (about 341K subscribers, average video about 600 minutes) in July 2025: about 40-70K views a day and an estimated $70-240 a day, implying about $1.75-5.25 RPM. These are third-party estimates. — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-much-sleep-sounds-channel-makes); [vidIQ SleepDroid](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UClKZ6IL7WhLm3DePEr_fHPw)
- Cat-calm channels (vidIQ snapshots, 2025):
  - Relax My Cat: about 294.6M views, 1.03M subscribers, 2.59K videos.
  - Calm Your Cat: about 211.4M views, 498K subscribers.
  - Purr-Purr: about 927M views, 1.97M subscribers, average video about 88.5 minutes. The content focus is unconfirmed.
  — [vidIQ Relax My Cat](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC89zrYB2M6mj00nHdHWD-EA); [vidIQ Calm Your Cat](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCseGhLmIfr9TSxw9xRafo1Q); [vidIQ Purr-Purr](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC0_1eG1YZQnuXc80zELWm_A)
- A small rain channel's 10-hour video had only about 1,086 views. Long uploads don't guarantee reach. — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/sleep-soundscape-youtube-channel)

### Inferences
- Example maths at a $3 RPM: 100K views a month is about US$300. Getting into the YouTube Partner Programme requires 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours. Long sleep videos rack up watch hours fast; this is a standard YPP rule but was not re-verified in this session.
- A safe format would be real, varied footage of the cat (changing scenes and lighting, an on-screen name and story, different sessions recorded, chapters) instead of one still image re-uploaded in different lengths. Mixing in Shorts of the same cat builds the brand that makes the long-form "authentic."

### Gaps
- YouTube Help pages couldn't be fetched to quote the official inauthentic-content examples directly.
- No verified Canadian-audience RPM was found.
- No specific channel was confirmed as "cat purring 10 hours."

## 6. Verdict: saturated or viable?

### Takeaway
Generic purring audio is saturated and commoditised: free CC0 and AI SFX, existing purr apps, many "Cat Purring Pt. X" streaming tracks, and big cat-calm YouTube channels. Streaming income is deliberately suppressed. It is viable only as part of a brand built around one real, named cat, where authenticity is the point of difference.

### Cited Findings
- Streaming is suppressed by 2-minute and 1,000-stream thresholds and an about-20% noise valuation. — [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2023/11/22/tech/spotify-slashes-royalties-functional-noise); [MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/changes-to-spotifys-royalty-model-including-the-1000-annual-streams-royalty-policy-are-officially-live)
- Free purr SFX are widely available. — [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects/cat-purring); [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/cat-purr-s0436.html)
- Large proven audience for cat-calming content on YouTube. — [vidIQ Relax My Cat](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC89zrYB2M6mj00nHdHWD-EA)

### Inferences
Best 1-3 channels for a creator with one real cat, in priority order:
1. **YouTube long-form plus Shorts.** This is the biggest demand pool. Real-footage purr sessions of a named cat meet the July 2025 authenticity test far better than static loops. Income is modest: roughly US$2-6 per 1,000 views once in YPP.
2. **Direct digital downloads on Gumroad or Payhip** (Etsy as secondary discovery). Sell premium long-form, high-res purr files and bundles to the audience the cat builds, so the creator keeps most of the revenue.
3. **Pond5 (30%, non-exclusive) or AudioJungle stock SFX** as a low-effort "upload once" sideline. Expect very small returns.

Treat streaming distribution as optional brand presence only, with rejection risk. Sleep apps are not a realistic self-serve channel.

### Gaps
- Realistic first-year income figures for a new purring brand could not be sourced. All income figures above are illustrative calculations from cited rates.
