# How cat influencers and faceless pet channels make money (2025-2026)

Context: a cat brand from Toronto with a YouTube channel of about 1,000 subscribers, not yet in the YouTube Partner Program (YPP), and no TikTok or Instagram for the cat yet. Research date: 5 Nov 2026.

Method note: this session could not reach several primary sources (support.google.com, support.tiktok.com, associates.amazon.ca, affiliate-program.amazon.com, chewy.com). Search snippets of those pages were used where available. Many figures come from third-party blogs and vendor guides, and they are flagged as such. Check primary pages before quoting any number publicly.

## 1. YouTube: YPP thresholds, RPM, Shorts, cat channel income, inauthentic-content policy

### Takeaway
At 1,000 subscribers the channel has passed the subscriber count for both YPP tiers. Ad revenue still needs 4,000 long-form watch hours, or 10M Shorts views, in 12 months. The 500-subscriber fan-funding tier (memberships, Super Thanks) needs 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views. Pet/animal RPM is mid-to-low, about $2-6 for long-form and pennies for Shorts, and below ASMR/sleep. AI-assisted videos stay monetizable only if they are not templated or mass-produced.

### Cited Findings
- **Fan-funding (expanded) YPP tier:** 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 3M public Shorts views in 90 days. It unlocks fan funding (channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks) and YouTube Shopping, but not ad revenue — [YouTube Official Blog](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/are-you-ready-to-make-money-on-youtube); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-relaxes-monetization-rules/)
- **Ad-revenue tier:** 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 long-form watch hours (12 months) or 10M Shorts views (90 days). Shorts watch time does not count toward the watch-hour total — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-relaxes-monetization-rules/); [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-partner-program-500-subscribers)
- **Unverified future change:** vidIQ says the ad-revenue thresholds rise to 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views from 1 Feb 2027, with the fan-funding tier unchanged. I found no official YouTube confirmation — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-relaxes-monetization-rules/)
- **How the Shorts revenue share works:**
  - Shorts ad revenue is pooled. Where music is used, part of the pool goes to music licensing: no music means all of the revenue tied to those views goes into the Creator Pool, and one track means 50% goes to music.
  - The pool is split by each creator's share of eligible Shorts views, and the creator keeps 45% of their allocation — [YouTube Help (Shorts monetization policies)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en-GB)
  - YouTube does not publish what share of total Shorts ad revenue enters the pool — [Buffer summary](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-monetization/); [CNBC 2022](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/09/20/youtube-will-share-revenue-with-shorts-creators-as-tiktok-surges.html)
- **Pet/animal long-form RPM, by source:**
  - About $2-6 RPM — [OutlierKit 2026](https://outlierkit.com/blog/highest-paying-youtube-niches)
  - Pet product reviews: $6-12 CPM (CPM, not RPM) — [vidIQ 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/most-profitable-youtube-niches/)
  - Animal Facts about $10.2 RPM and Animal Rescue about $7.3 RPM (faceless-niche dataset, method unclear) — [Autonolab Sept 2026](https://blog.autonolab.com/blog/2026-09-07-youtube-rpm-by-niche-98-faceless-niches-data/)
- **Pet Shorts RPM:** about $0.02-0.08 per 1,000 views — [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-rpm-pets-niche)
- **ASMR/sleep RPM, by source:**
  - Sleep/Healing Soundscapes average $10.92 RPM, with the top 10% above $15 — [Autonolab 2026](https://blog.autonolab.com/blog/2026-09-07-youtube-rpm-by-niche-98-faceless-niches-data/)
  - Relaxation & Sleep Music $4-10 CPM — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/most-profitable-youtube-niches/)
  - Other lists put ASMR anywhere from about $3.50 to $12 RPM — [virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/highest-rpm-niches-on-youtube); [nichepursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/youtube-rpm/)
  - These sources disagree widely.
- **Cat channel income examples:**
  - Flippa listing: a monetized cat channel with about 95K subscribers and 115K monthly views made about $669-769/month revenue (Feb-Jun 2026) and about $653/month profit. This is seller-reported. It works out to roughly $6 per 1,000 views — [Flippa](https://flippa.com/13362438-established-monetized-cat-youtube-channel-with-95k-subscribers-115k-monthly-views-evergreen-content-strong-u-s-traffic-and-steady-revenue)
  - vidIQ's tool estimates for cat channels range widely: 167K subscribers at $6K-18K/month, 788K subscribers at $2.6K-7.9K/month, 496K subscribers at $10.5K-31.6K/month. The tool does not explain its method — [vidIQ stats pages](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCpyuz75AKPRJ5VRNwHzQf6Q)
  - A "Calm Your Cat" relaxing music/TV-for-cats channel exists in this niche, which links cat content to the higher-RPM sleep/relax format — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCseGhLmIfr9TSxw9xRafo1Q)
- **July 2025 inauthentic-content update (effective 15 July 2025):**
  - YouTube renamed the "repetitious content" YPP guideline to "inauthentic content". It called this a minor clarification, and said mass-produced or repetitive content was already ineligible.
  - Using AI to improve content stays eligible if the content meets all other policies.
  - Examples of what is out: videos made from a single template with minimal variation, and slideshows without meaningful narration or commentary.
  - Reviews can apply to whole channels.
  - Sources: [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/); [subsub](https://www.subsub.io/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-2025)

### Inferences
- With 1,000 subscribers, the binding constraint is watch time, not subscribers. A plausible first target is the fan-funding tier (3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views).
- At a pet RPM of about $3-6, 100K monthly long-form views would earn roughly $300-600/month in ads. The Flippa example (about $6 per 1K views) supports the upper end for US-heavy evergreen cat content.
- Pet Shorts ad revenue is close to zero: 1M Shorts views at $0.02-0.08 RPM is about $20-80. Shorts are better treated as a discovery funnel.
- Mixing in a "relaxing video for cats / cat ASMR" long-form format could lift RPM toward the sleep/ASMR range. No source directly measures RPM for cat-ASMR hybrids.
- Real cat footage with original narration or story is low-risk under the inauthentic-content policy. AI-generated cat clips, templated compilations or voice-over slideshows are at risk of channel-level demonetization.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the YouTube Help YPP page (support.google.com was blocked) to confirm the current thresholds or the 2027 change.
- No source in this session gives the long-form ad revenue share (widely reported as 55%). It is unverified here.
- No credible Tubefilter or creator-interview case study on a small cat channel's income was found. Only the 2014 Henri Le Chat Noir piece came up, with no income data.
- No Canada-specific RPM data was found. Canadian viewers typically earn less than US viewers, but this is unverified here.

## 2. TikTok Creator Rewards, TikTok Shop affiliate, Instagram/Facebook monetization (Canada)

### Takeaway
Canada's eligibility for TikTok Creator Rewards is contested. Older reporting (CBC) and 2025 lists say Canada is excluded, while some 2026 blogs list it. As of September 2026, TikTok Shop and its affiliate program had not officially launched in Canada. Facebook Content Monetization reportedly lists Canada but is invite-only. No reliable 2026 Instagram payout data was found.

### Cited Findings
- **CBC:** Canada is not among the countries that qualify for TikTok's Creator Rewards Program — [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7235487)
- **2025 petition:** Canadian creators cannot access the Creator Fund, Creativity Program or any formal ad revenue share — [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/canadian-tiktok-creators-deserve-to-be-paid-paycanadiancreators)
- **Conflicting 2026 blogs:** Some list Canada as eligible, with requirements of 18+, 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in the last 30 days. Videos must be over 1 minute (general program requirement) — [ttcalculator 2026](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/); [canadian-followers](https://canadian-followers.pages.dev/resources/how-much-does-tiktok-pay-canada/); [makeinfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-how-organic-monetization-works-in-2026)
  - These are commercial blogs of low reliability.
- **TikTok Effect Creator Rewards** (for AR effects, not video) expanded to Canada in 2024 — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/03/13/tiktok-effect-creator-rewards-monetization-program-requirements/)
- **TikTok Shop in Canada:**
  - A February 2026 Metricool (French) guide says no official date or timeline had been given — [Metricool FR](https://metricool.com/fr/monetisation-tiktok-canada/)
  - A September 2026 guide still reports no official Canadian launch — [socialagechecker](https://socialagechecker.net/blog/make-money-on-tiktok-in-canada/)
  - One expansion roundup claims a full Canada launch in January 2026. This is unverified and conflicts with the sources above — [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/blog/tiktok-shop-expansion-2026/)
  - TikTok was hiring a "Commerce Creator Manager" in Toronto, which suggests preparation — [Net Influencer jobs](https://www.netinfluencer.com/jobs/tiktok-commerce-creator-manager-at-tiktok-toronto/)
- **Regulatory context:** in March 2026 Ottawa reportedly reversed its wind-down order and let TikTok keep operating in Canada under data-security conditions — [socialagechecker](https://socialagechecker.net/blog/make-money-on-tiktok-in-canada/) (secondary)
- **TikTok Shop affiliate in the US:** creators earn commission on physical products sold through TikTok Shop — [Digistore24 2026](https://www.digistore24.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-tiktok/). Pet-category commission rates were not found.
- **Facebook Content Monetization:**
  - Several 2026 guides say Canada is on the eligible-country list, but the program is invite-only, with the invitation shown in the Professional Dashboard — [shortsync 2026](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/facebook-content-monetization-program-2026); [NOW Toronto](https://nowtoronto.com/news/facebook-is-offering-creators-money-to-post-heres-how-canadians-can-apply/)
  - Blogs conflict on follower and watch-time thresholds — [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/facebook-video-monetization-requirements)

### Inferences
- A Toronto-based creator should not plan around TikTok platform payouts or TikTok Shop affiliate income in the near term. TikTok is best used for audience growth and as a portfolio for brand deals and UGC.
- Facebook's invite-only Content Monetization may be the most realistic Meta payout for a Canadian pet page. Pet clips perform well on Facebook Reels, but no pet-specific Facebook RPM data was found.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch TikTok's official Creator Rewards help page (support.tiktok.com was blocked) to confirm the eligible-country list.
- No official TikTok Shop Canada announcement was found.
- No reliable source on Instagram's 2026 creator payout programs (bonuses, subscriptions, gifts) for Canada or for animal content was found.
- No US TikTok Shop pet-category commission benchmark was found.

## 3. Affiliate programs: Amazon, Chewy, Petco/PetSmart/Pet Valu, pet brands in Canada

### Takeaway
Retailer affiliate commissions for pet products are low (about 2-4%), so they pay meaningfully only at volume. High-ticket cat tech pays far better per sale: Litter-Robot Canada lists 8% on an average order of $550+, or about $44+ per sale. Chewy Canada has an official affiliate program at 4%.

### Cited Findings
- **Amazon.com (US):** the standard fee schedule uses fixed per-category rates, with 4% for "all other categories" — [Amazon Associates US](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/welcome/compensation)
  - A third-party blog puts pet supplies at roughly 3-4% — [redefineweb](https://redefineweb.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-for-pet-products/)
  - The exact current US pet rate was not confirmed in this session.
- **Amazon UK:** Pet Supplies sits in the 3% tier — [Amazon UK Associates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ). This is shown as a reference point only.
- **Amazon.ca:** commission is paid under its Commission Income Schedule, calculated on the sale price — [Amazon.ca Associates help](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/GWJ4AHCH7U5LCL86)
  - The Canadian pet rate was not retrievable. The schedule is at [associates.amazon.ca](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ).
- **Amazon Influencer Program (US):**
  - Onsite video and storefront commissions reportedly range from 0% to 5% by category — [viralvue](https://viralvue.com/blog/if-you-are-eyeing-the-amazon-influencer-program-the-first-question-is-simple-what-could-you-actually-earn-the-honest-answer-is-that-earnings-vary-a-lot-but-you-can-estimate-them-once-you-know-how-amazon-pays.)
  - 2026 range of 0.75% (Toys) to 4% (Luxury Beauty), and 3 uploaded videos are needed to unlock onsite commissions — [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/how-to-make-amazon-shoppable-videos)
  - Sources conflict on whether onsite rates match standard Associates rates, are narrower, or are about half of them — [Ways to Wealth](https://www.thewaystowealth.com/amazon-influencer-program/); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/amazon-influencer-program/); [EntreResource](https://entreresource.com/amazon-influencer-program/)
  - Amazon reportedly doubled rates in 13 categories for Prime Day, 1-20 July 2025 — [Ways to Wealth](https://www.thewaystowealth.com/amazon-influencer-program/)
- **Chewy Canada:** official affiliate page; 4% commission on all Chewy Canada orders — [Chewy CA affiliate](https://www.chewy.com/ca/app/content/affiliate)
  - The program runs via Partnerize (US) — [Partnerize](https://partnerize.com/resources/blog/chewy-affiliate-program-spotlight-and-sign-up-information)
- **Petco:**
  - Directories conflict: 2% — [creator-hero](https://www.creator-hero.com/fr/blog/petco-affiliate-program-in-depth-review-pros-and-cons); 4% US-only — [postaffiliatepro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/petco-affiliate-program/); "10%" — [cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/high-paying-pet-affiliate-programs/)
  - No Canadian Petco program was found.
- **Pet Valu (Canada):** 2% per sale with a 7-day cookie, via FlexOffers/Lasso — [getlasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/pet-valu-ca/); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/pet-valu-ca-affiliate-program/). Third-party, unverified.
- **Litter-Robot (Whisker) Canada:**
  - Affiliate page: average order $550+, 90-day cookie, 8% commission. Aimed at publishers, content creators and influencers; coupon sites and networks excluded — [Litter-Robot CA affiliates](https://litter-robot.com/ca/affiliates.html)
  - Whisker's partnerships page accepts US and Canadian residents. Influencers get a $25-off referral code. It cites a $650+ new-customer average order with an unstated rate — [Whisker partnerships](https://www.whisker.com/partnerships)
  - The program runs on Impact — [affilitizer](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/litter-robot.com)
  - Canadian launch prices (June 2026): Litter-Robot 5 at CA$1,199 and EVO at CA$899 — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260601926486/en/Whisker-Launches-Litter-Robot-5-Litter-Robot-EVO-and-LitterHopper-5-in-Canada)

### Inferences
- **Worked example:** a CA$30 cat-supply basket at 3-4% earns about CA$1 per order, so 100 orders a month is about CA$100.
  - A single Litter-Robot 5 sale at 8% of CA$1,199 is about CA$96.
  - For a small cat channel, one or two high-ticket "honest review / day-in-the-life" affiliate placements (litter robot, fountain, camera feeder) likely beat dozens of low-ticket Amazon links.
- Amazon.ca Associates is available to Canadian creators. The US Amazon Influencer Program normally requires a US-facing audience and tax setup (unverified for Canadian residents).

- It is unknown whether the Amazon Influencer Program and its onsite commissions are open on Amazon.ca.
- No PetSmart affiliate details were found.
- No Catit, PetSafe or Drinkwell (fountain) programs were found, nor any cat food subscription programs in Canada (e.g. Smalls, Kabo).

## 4. Brand deals and UGC: rates by tier and platforms that accept small pet creators

### Takeaway
Nano pet creators (1K-10K) usually earn about $20-250 per sponsored post; most charge under $100. Micro creators (10K-100K) earn about $250-1,500. Pet UGC (content delivered to the brand, not posted to the creator's audience) typically pays about $80-300 per video, and the brand does not require a large following.

### Cited Findings
- **Dogfluence benchmark (June 2026):** 34% of pet influencers charge under $50 per sponsored post and 57% charge up to $100. The rest range from $100 to over $1,000 — [Dogfluence](https://dogfluence.com/blog/dog-influencer-rate-benchmarks)
- **LaunchPoint (2026):**
  - US pet micro-influencers: $250-1,500 per post. Nano: typically under $250 — [LaunchPoint micro](https://www.launchpointhq.com/guides/rates/how-much-do-pets-micro-influencers-charge); [LaunchPoint nano](https://www.launchpointhq.com/guides/rates/how-much-do-pets-nano-influencers-charge)
  - Pet UGC: about $150-300 per ad-ready video — [LaunchPoint UGC](https://www.launchpointhq.com/guides/rates/how-much-do-pets-ugc-creators-charge)
- **Elev8or:**
  - Pet YouTube nano (1K-10K): $94-468 per post — [Elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/find/youtube-pets-influencer-rates)
  - Pet UGC: $80-500 per video depending on length, usage rights and revisions. Pet sits at the low end of UGC pricing across niches — [Elev8or pets UGC](https://www.elev8or.io/creators/ugc-creators/pets); [Elev8or rate card](https://www.elev8or.io/blog/ugc-rate-card)
- **General nano Instagram benchmarks:** $20-100 per post (Fiverr 2025; Meltwater 2026) — [Fiverr](https://block.fiverr.com/resources/guides/digital-marketing/influencer-marketing-cost); [Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/influencer-marketing-costs-rates-pricing)
- **General UGC benchmarks:**
  - About $200 per short video — [Billo 2025](https://billo.app/blog/ugc-rates/)
  - Median offer $100 per video; half fall between $50 and $200 — [Knowlify citing Pitchlo](https://knowlify.com/articles/ugc-creator-rates)
  - Collabstr's average spend per UGC creator is $177.68 (not pet-specific) — [Collabstr](https://collabstr.com/blog/ugc-trends-platforms-explained)

### Inferences
- At 1K YouTube subscribers and no TikTok or Instagram yet, paid posts realistically pay gifted product up to about $100-250. UGC packages (for example, 3 videos at $100-200 each) are the fastest cash route, because pay depends on footage quality, not reach.
- Marketplaces such as Collabstr, Elev8or, Billo, Insense and Influee accept small creators. Whether each one onboards Canadian creators was not verified.

### Gaps
- No Influencer Marketing Hub, Later or Shopify pet-specific 2025-2026 rate report was retrieved.
- No information on Aspire or pet-specialist agencies (e.g. "Petfluencer" agencies, The Dog Agency) and their minimum follower requirements.
- No Canadian (CAD) rate data was found.

## 5. Licensing viral pet clips (Jukin/Trusted Media Brands, ViralHog, Newsflare, Storyful)

### Takeaway
Licensing agencies typically split revenue about 50/50, and sometimes 60/40 or 70/30 in the creator's favour. They often take exclusive or perpetual rights. Earnings are lumpy: a single viral clip may earn tens to a few hundred dollars, and a few earn much more. Treat licensing as a bonus for a breakout clip, not a steady income stream.

### Cited Findings
- **ViralHog:** most licences are revenue-share deals — [Digiday/CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/turning-viral-videos-into-money-machines)
  - Reported terms: 50% of net, $75 minimum payout, monthly — [MoneyPantry](https://moneypantry.com/sell-videos-to-media/)
- **Newsflare:** sources conflict on the split.
  - 50/50 — [ViralPress](https://viralpress.com/knowledge-base/what-are-the-top-five-video-licensing-agencies-in-the-world-right-now)
  - 45% to the creator (older report) — [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/news/agency-newsflare-doubles-down-on-ugc-with-5m-investment/)
- **Jukin Licensing** (owned by Trusted Media Brands):
  - Reported 50% split, $100 minimum, monthly payouts — [MoneyPantry](https://moneypantry.com/sell-videos-to-media/)
  - Individual deals reported at 70% and 60% to the creator, and Jukin may take rights in perpetuity — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/turning-viral-videos-into-money-machines)
  - Jukin reported $25M in total royalty payments by 2020 — [SoCalTech 2020](https://socaltech.com/jukin_media_hits___m_in_royalty_payments_from_viral_videos/s-0080031.html) (old data)
- **Creator anecdote:** one creator sold a viral video licence for €340 — [Xataka](https://www.xataka.com/especiales/vendi-video-viral-internet-esto-fue-que-me-pagaron)
- **Criticism:** Jukin's practices (aggressive claims, exclusive rights) have drawn backlash — [Reclaim the Net](https://reclaimthenet.org/jukin-media-twitter-backlash-complaints/); [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/one-company-reshaped-kind-ruined-210858857.html)

### Inferences
- **Risk for a brand channel:** an exclusive licence can stop the creator from monetizing their own clip, or lead to Content ID claims against their own uploads. Prefer non-exclusive deals, or license only clips that sit outside the channel's core content.

### Gaps
- No current (2025-2026) official creator terms from ViralHog, Newsflare, Jukin or Storyful were retrieved.
- No Storyful payout data was found.
- No reliable average-earnings figure per licensed pet clip, beyond anecdotes, was found.

## 6. Memberships, Patreon and YouTube memberships for pet channels

### Takeaway
YouTube channel memberships, Super Thanks and Super Chat open at the 500-subscriber fan-funding tier once 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views are reached. Fan funding depends on a highly engaged community and rarely earns meaningful money at small scale. No pet-specific membership or Patreon earnings data was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Fan-funding tier:** channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers and Super Thanks become available at 500 subscribers plus the watch-time or Shorts-views threshold — [YouTube Official Blog](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/are-you-ready-to-make-money-on-youtube)
- **Small-scale reality:** 500 subscribers rarely turns into meaningful revenue, because fan funding depends on an engaged community — [vidIQ](https://stg.vidiq.com/blog/post/get-500-youtube-subscribers-fan-funding/)

### Inferences
- For a cat brand, memberships work best with clear perks: name the next foster kitten, exclusive outtakes, a monthly live stream with the cat, or the cat's "birthday" fundraiser. Expect low single-digit percentages of viewers to convert at most. No source in this session supports a specific conversion rate.

### Gaps
- No Patreon or YouTube membership earnings data was found for pet channels, and no case study.
- YouTube's revenue share on memberships and Super Thanks (commonly cited as 70% to the creator) is not confirmed by a source in this session.
