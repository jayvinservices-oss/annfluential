# Etsy and Print-on-Demand Cat Products in 2026: Demand, Fees, AI Rules and Earnings

Method note (5 Nov 2026): WebFetch could not reach etsy.com or several other sites from this session (DNS blocked). All findings below come from web-search result summaries. Most come from third-party blogs, calculators and vendor sites, plus some primary sources (SEC filing, ASX announcements, Redbubble blog). Before quoting any figure to a customer or putting it in a pricing sheet, check it against Etsy's official pages (etsy.com/ca/legal/fees and etsy.com/legal/creativity).

## Q1. Etsy 2025-2026: fees, creativity standards, AI disclosure, production partners, crackdowns

### Takeaway
Etsy still allows AI-assisted art and print-on-demand, but only with disclosure. The seller must have a real creative role, and production partners must be disclosed. The 22 October 2026 update banned bot-generated listings, AI prompt bundles and AI-generated patterns and recipes. It also requires at least one unedited photo of the real item for physical goods and lets buyers flag suspected AI listings. Base fees run about 10-13% per sale for a Canadian seller, rising to about 25% or more on sales attributed to Offsite Ads.

### Cited Findings
**Fees**
- Core fees: a US$0.20 listing fee (per 4-month listing or renewal), a 6.5% transaction fee, and payment processing of 3% + $0.25. The transaction fee applies to the full order total including shipping. — [Voolist](https://voolist.com/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026); [ecomcalctools, Etsy Canada](https://ecomcalctools.com/blog/fees-etsy/etsy-regional-canada/); [Promptless Press](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-fees-explained)
- One guide gives Canadian payment processing as 3% + CAD $0.25, but the same guide is inconsistent about the currency, so this needs verifying. — [ecom-tools-hub](https://ecom-tools-hub.vercel.app/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026)
- Canadian regulatory operating fee: most sources list 1.15% of the order total, including shipping. — [Printful Canada](https://www.printful.com/ca/blog/how-much-does-etsy-take-per-sale); [Crosslist](https://crosslist.com/blog/etsy-review). One calculator lists Canada at 0.50%. — [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/free-tools/etsy-fee-calculator). The sources conflict, and 1.15% is the majority figure.
- Offsite Ads: shops with under US$10,000 in sales over the past 365 days pay 15% on attributed sales and can opt out. Shops at or above $10,000 must take part, at 12%. A shop that opted out stays enrolled at 12% for the life of the shop once it crosses $10,000. The fee is reportedly capped at US$100 per order. A sale counts as attributed if it happens within 30 days of an ad click. — [Etsy CA fee page via search snippet](https://etsy.com/ca/legal/fees); [Printful UK](https://www.printful.com/uk/blog/how-much-does-etsy-take-per-sale); [GetOneCart](https://www.getonecart.com/tools/etsy-fee-calculator/)
- Total effective fees are about 10-13% per standard sale and about 25% when Offsite Ads apply. — [Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees); [Calcmatic](https://calcmatic.pages.dev/articles/etsy-fees-guide-2026/)

**Creativity standards and production partners**
- Under "Designed by a seller", Etsy allows "seller-prompted AI creations", meaning items generated with AI tools from the seller's own original prompts. Etsy's own example is a custom pet portrait made with AI tools. — [Etsy Creativity Standards / House Rules](https://etsy.com/legal/creativity) (via search snippet); [Etsy CA guidelines](https://www.etsy.com/ca/new-guidelines)
- Sellers must disclose a production partner and give accurate ship-from information. Etsy's example of an allowed production-partner item is "a seller's unique, original design, printed onto a blanket by a production partner", which is a print-on-demand model. — [Etsy House Rules](https://www.etsy.com/new-guidelines)
- Third-party guides say AI use must be disclosed in the listing, and that missing disclosure is a common reason listings are removed. Stock images are not allowed, except for production-partner mockups. — [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards); [Inkfluence AI](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-explained-2026)
- In the 10 June 2025 rewrite, Etsy reportedly removed an allowance for "templated design or pattern". Through 2025 the focus was on enforcing the existing standards more consistently. — [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards); [BulkMockup](https://www.bulkmockup.com/etsy-policy-changes/)
- One outlet claims more than 17,000 listings were removed in early 2025 for AI-disclosure violations. This is a single unverified figure. — [Artomate](https://www.artomate.app/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026)

**The 22 October 2026 update**
- Etsy now removes bot-generated listings with no human involvement. AI use must be disclosed and cannot be passed off as handmade. Buyers can flag listings they suspect are AI-generated, but these reports will be used to gather information at first rather than to trigger enforcement. Detection alerts appear while a seller creates a listing, and detection is expected to reach existing listings later. Removed listings can be appealed through the Policy Violations page in Shop Manager. — [Digital Commerce 360, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/26/etsy-ai-policy/amp/)
- The update extends the July 2024 ban on AI prompt bundles to AI-generated patterns, recipes and how-to instructions, even when disclosed. — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/10/etsy-cracks-down-on-ai-slop-bans-ai-generated-patterns-and-recipes-and-tightens-photo-rules/); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/)
- Most listings must include at least one unaltered photo of the real item. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/etsy-forces-sellers-to-show-one-unaltered-photo-in-every-listing/); [FindSkill](https://findskill.ai/blog/etsy-ai-rules-october-2026-seller-guide/)
- Digital downloads are largely exempt from the photo rule but must include a visual preview or excerpt of what buyers receive. AI art and AI-made digital downloads remain allowed when the seller has a creative role and discloses AI use. Reselling untouched templates or clip-art bundles is prohibited. — [Inkfluence AI, Oct 2026](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/etsy-ai-rules-update-october-2026); [iscompliant](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-ai-digital-downloads)
- Etsy also expanded its rights to use seller content for AI training and tightened rules on third-party AI use. — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-expands-ai-training-rights/) (headline only, details not reviewed)
- Sellers report uneven enforcement: real handmade listings get pulled while AI-flooded listings stay visible in search. — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/etsy-bans-ai-generated-listings-but-sellers-say-it-still-isnt-working/); [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/etsy-calls-itself-human-centered-its-sellers-have-a-more-complicated-story-to-tell/)
- Sources disagree on the grace period. One says no deadline was given; a low-quality source claims 60 days. — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/26/etsy-ai-policy/amp/)

**Platform health**
- Q3 2026 (quarter to 30 Sep 2026): about 87.5M trailing-12-month active buyers, up 1% YoY and Etsy's first YoY buyer growth in two years. About 5.8M active sellers, up 6.5%. GMS about US$2.6B, up 8.8%. GMS per active buyer US$126, up 3.9%. Revenue US$692.5M. Full-year 2026 GMS guided to about +7%. — [Etsy Q3 2026 shareholder letter, SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001370637/000137063726000090/q326shareholderletter.htm); [Finviz](https://finviz.com/news/410131/etsy-shares-rise-85-as-q3-earnings-beat-forecasts-and-buyer-growth-returns)
- The year before, in Q3 2025, active buyers fell 5% to 86.6M and active sellers fell 10.9% to 5.5M. — [Penluma](https://penluma.com/blog/make-money-with-ai/16-print-on-demand-etsy/); [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/48502613)

### Inferences
- Ace is a real cat, which is a compliance advantage for this brand. Real photos, original drawings and AI-assisted designs based on Ace, disclosed as such, fit "designed by a seller". A print-on-demand blanket or mug with an original Ace design is exactly Etsy's own example of an allowed production-partner item.
- Fee budget per sale for a Canadian seller: 6.5% transaction + about 3% + $0.25 processing + about 1.15% regulatory fee is roughly 11% + $0.45 including the listing fee. Add 15% on any sale attributed to Offsite Ads. Opting out while under US$10k keeps margins predictable, but the 12% lifetime enrolment applies after crossing the threshold.
- After October 2026, the highest-risk cat products are AI-made printables with instructions, such as colouring or pattern books with how-to content, and anything listed without disclosure or without a real photo of the item.

### Gaps
- Etsy's official Canadian fee page and Creativity Standards could not be fetched directly. The exact CAD figures for the listing fee, processing fee, regulatory fee and currency conversion fee (commonly cited at 2.5% elsewhere, not verified here) should be confirmed on Etsy.
- No primary Etsy figure was found on how many listings were removed in 2025-2026.

## Q2. Demand signals for cat and pet niches

### Takeaway
Pet portraits, cat memorial items and "cat mom" gifts are clearly established, high-review categories on Etsy, and "cat" has been a strong, high-click-through keyword in eRank's trend data. Q4 gifting is when memorial and portrait products get positioned. No independent sales-volume or seasonality data specific to cats was found.

### Cited Findings
- eRank ranked "cat" as Etsy keyword #19 in August 2024, up from #169, with a 145% 12-month average CTR. In Q1 2025 it was among the high-CTR top-50 keywords. — [eRank Aug 2024](https://help.erank.com/blog/trend-report/top-keywords-on-etsy-august-2024/); [eRank Q1 2025](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-q1-2025/)
- Stickers made the monthly top 20 in most months of 2023, and "wall art" made all but two of the monthly top-20 lists. — [eRank 2023 review](https://help.erank.com/blog/trend-report/eranks-2023-etsy-shopper-search-trends-review-learn-what-keywords-are-trending/)
- "Medieval cat" gets about 27,100 monthly searches against only 4,786 listings, which looks like a lower-competition, theme-specific niche. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/medieval-cat)
- Etsy's "cat mom gifts" category page lists custom pet portraits and personalised cat memorial garden stones among its "bestselling" items. — [Etsy cat mom gifts](https://www.etsy.com/market/cat_mom_gifts)
- Memorial jewellery leads on review counts. One cat necklace shows about 14.8k reviews and a pet-loss capsule necklace about 52.8k. Memorial items are positioned for Christmas, for example memorial cat ornaments and a "tuxedo cat memorial suncatcher" sold as a Christmas gift for a cat mom. Realistic memorial plush cats are listed around US$129. — [Etsy cat memorial gift](https://www.etsy.com/market/cat_memorial_gift); [Etsy pet memorial gift](https://www.etsy.com/market/pet_memorial_gift)
- Examples of large pet-portrait shops: PetPortraitArtShop (Florida) displays about 7.9k sales (cached page). EtsyHunt publishes a top-100 best-selling dog portrait list with sales and revenue estimates. — [Etsy PetPortraitArtShop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/PetPortraitArtShop); [EtsyHunt](https://ehunt.ai/etsy-competitor-research/best-etsy-dog-portraits)
- One promotional guide claims established pet-portrait shops make 50-300 sales a month. It gives no method. — [chatimg.ai](https://chatimg.ai/en/blog/ai-pet-portrait-etsy-commercial-guide)
- EverBee sales numbers are estimates reverse-engineered from review counts, listing age and history, not exports from Etsy. — [StableCommerce](https://stablecommerce.ai/blog/everbee-review-etsy); [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/guides/how-to-use-everbee)

### Inferences
- "Tuxedo cat" is already used as a product qualifier in memorial listings. Coat-specific products (tuxedo, black-and-white cat) are a plausible niche for an Ace-branded shop, much like "medieval cat".
- Memorial and portrait gifts peak around the holidays and Mother's Day. The "cat mom" framing supports Mother's Day as a second seasonal peak, but this is inferred, not measured.

### Gaps
- No search-volume or competition data was found specifically for "cat calendar", "cat stickers", "tuxedo cat" or "cat wall art". A paid eRank, EverBee or Marmalead lookup is needed.
- No verified ranking of top cat-specific shops by sales count.
- No measured seasonality curves.

## Q3. Saturation and price compression

### Takeaway
The broad terms are saturated: wall art has about 15 million competing listings. AI pet portraits have pushed the bottom of the market down to roughly $5-25 for digital files, while hand-painted work stays at $150-300+. Printables are crowded at the category level but still winnable in narrow keyword niches.

### Cited Findings
- "Wall art" averages about 52,685 monthly searches with about 15.2 million competing Etsy listings. A new listing has "almost no shot" at page one. — [AutoKeyWorder](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/best-etsy-seo-tools/); [eRank Trend Buzz](https://members.erank.com/trend-buzz/etsy/us/weekly)
- "Cat tee" gets about 18,100 monthly searches against about 300,443 listings. — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/cat-tee)
- eRank's absolute search volumes can differ substantially from Etsy's own figures. For "printable wall art", Etsy showed about 21,300 searches over 30 days versus about 8,523 in eRank. — [AutoKeyWorder](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/best-etsy-seo-tools/)
- Pet portrait pricing, mostly from blogs written by AI pet-portrait vendors:
  - Etsy digital portraits start around $25, with a median of about $50-80. Hand-painted originals run $300+.
  - AI apps charge $5-50, and one AI service charges $19.99 for a digital file and $79.99 for a canvas.
  - Etsy artists charge $15-50 per extra pet.
  — [PetCanvas](https://create.petcanvas.art/blog/how-much-does-pet-portrait-cost); [PawFav](https://www.pawfav.ai/blog/custom-dog-cat-portrait)
- Etsy's "AI pet portrait custom" search pages show many AI listings discounted 45-60%. — [Etsy market page](https://www.etsy.com/market/ai_pet_portrait_custom)
- Printables are rated "very high" saturation, with planners and quote wall art the most crowded. A counterpoint argues the category is not saturated, only specific keywords are. — [Anlyzo](https://anlyzo.com/blog/is-selling-printables-on-etsy-saturated); [Insight Agent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/selling-printables-on-etsy)
- Memorial listings commonly show 30-50% "sale" discounts. — [Etsy cat memorial gift](https://www.etsy.com/market/cat_memorial_gift)

### Inferences
- Generic AI pet portraits compete with standalone AI apps (PawFav, PetCanvas and similar) priced at $5-20. That makes a low-price AI portrait service on Etsy a race to the bottom. Differentiation has to come from hand-finishing, a distinctive style, or bundling with a physical product.
- A real cat with an audience (Ace) acts as a brand moat that generic AI art lacks. Selling "Ace" products is less exposed to price compression than selling "any cat" products.

### Gaps
- No time-series data on falling prices. The compression is inferred from current price spreads and discount banners.

## Q4. Alternatives: royalties and 2025-2026 status

### Takeaway
Marketplace print-on-demand royalties are thin: Zazzle defaults to 5%, Society6 about 10%, TeePublic pays $2-4 per tee, and Amazon Merch introduced lower tiers in June 2026. Redbubble is shrinking and added an artist fee in September 2025. Running your own channel (Shopify + Printify, or Gumroad for digital) gives better margins but needs your own traffic, which Ace's social following could supply.

### Cited Findings
- **Redbubble / Articore:**
  - FY25 (to June 2025) marketplace revenue fell 10% to A$379.1M. Redbubble alone fell 19%, to A$194.7M, on weaker organic traffic, while TeePublic grew. Quarter to December 2025 marketplace revenue fell 3.2% to A$138.4M, an improving trend. — [Ragtrader](https://www.ragtrader.com.au/news/redbubble-parent-s-fy25-sales-down-amid-board-stoush); [Internet Retailing](https://internetretailing.com.au/articore-posts-best-q4-in-five-years-amid-turnaround/); [ASX announcement](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20250815/pdf/06mxp2802r3t9b.pdf)
  - A new artist fee structure took effect in September 2025. Blogs describe tiers of 50% of monthly earnings for Standard, 20% for Premium and 0% for Pro, reportedly capped at $150 per period. These are secondary sources. — [Trendlytic](https://www.trendlytic.io/blog/is-redbubble-worth-it); [Lavaritte](https://lavaritte.com/blogs/redbubble-for-artists-review/)
  - Payout threshold: Redbubble says it drops from $20 to $10 from 1 July 2026, yearly payments are removed, and artists must meet the monthly threshold to be paid. — [Redbubble blog](https://blog.redbubble.com/2024/12/changes-to-yearly-artist-payments/)
- **TeePublic:** flat per-tee royalties. Artisan tier: $4 at full price, $2 on sale. Apprentice tier: $3 and $1. No change found as of October 2026, and no account fee. — [Trendlytic](https://www.trendlytic.io/blog/teepublic-vs-redbubble)
- **Society6:** royalty structure updated in March 2025, with a 10% baseline on most products. No shutdown reported. — [Scaylr](https://scaylr.io/blog/society6-vs-redbubble)
- **Amazon Merch on Demand:** moved to a three-tier royalty system on 1 June 2026, with tiers based on the share of traffic sellers bring from outside Amazon.
  - Per-tee figures for a US$19.99 shirt conflict: $2.44 at the base Creator tier versus $4.88 under the old flat structure, and other estimates of $3.25-4.63.
  - The royalty is list price minus production cost, a 15% referral fee and taxes.
  — [MerchTitans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-june-2026); [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-2026/)
- **Zazzle:** sellers set their own royalty from a default of 5% up to 99%, with Zazzle Heart products locked at 5%. A $25 tee at 5% earns about $1.25. Another Fourthwall article says 10-15% is typical, which conflicts. — [Fourthwall](https://fourthwall.com/blog/best-alternatives-to-zazzle-for-print-on-demand)
- **Printify:** free plan available. Premium rose from $29 to $39 a month on 17 February 2026; annual billing stays at $299 a year. Premium gives up to about 20% off product costs. — [eCommerceCEO](https://www.ecommerceceo.com/printify-pricing/); [Traksource](https://traksource.com/printify-review/)
- **Gumroad:**
  - Direct or profile sales: 10% + $0.50, with card processing (about 2.9% + $0.30) reportedly charged on top.
  - Discover marketplace sales: 30% all-in.
  - One unverified source claims a 5% tier above $20k a month.
  — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/gumroad-fees-explained); [Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees); [ChargePanda](https://www.chargepanda.com/blog/post/why-self-hosted-beats-gumroad-for-serious-creators)

### Inferences
- For Ace merchandise, marketplace print-on-demand (Redbubble, TeePublic, Society6, Zazzle) works as a low-effort "upload once" side channel only. Expect $1-4 per sale.
- Etsy plus a Printify or Printful integration, or Shopify plus Printify driven by Ace's social audience, keeps far more margin per item.
- Gumroad or a Shopify digital store suits cat printables and wallpapers sold to an existing audience. Gumroad's Discover cut of 30% is steep.

### Gaps
- Shopify plan pricing in CAD, Printify and Printful base costs or shipping to Canada, and cross-border duties were not researched.
- No official Redbubble help-centre confirmation of the fee tiers.
- TeePublic's own status page was not reviewed.

## Q5. Best effort-to-income products for a solo seller with AI help, and what to avoid

### Takeaway
Most Etsy sellers earn very little. One source puts it at about 65% of sellers making under $100 a year. Digital downloads have the best margins but the worst saturation. Print-on-demand nets about 15-30% after fees and ads. The best effort-to-income bets for a real-cat brand are: original, Ace-branded designs with real photos; personalised pet portraits that are hand-finished or have a distinctive style (avoid generic AI); and memorial and gift products positioned for Q4 and Mother's Day. Avoid generic AI wall art and printables, AI pattern or how-to products, and undisclosed AI.

### Cited Findings
- About 65% of Etsy sellers make under $100 a year and about 5% treat Etsy as their main income. Reaching the first $1,000 takes about 165 days. — [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make-2026)
- Promotional sources claim beginners make $200-1,000 a month and established digital sellers with 100+ products make $2,000-10,000 a month. These claims are inflated and come from vendors with an interest in selling the model. — [SideQuestHustle](https://sidequesthustle.com/guides/etsy-digital-products-guide-2026); [Insight Agent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make)
- Margins: digital downloads keep about 90-95% of revenue by one estimate, or about 50-70% once ads and processing are included by another. Print-on-demand typically nets 15-30%. — [ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make-2026); [Penluma](https://penluma.com/blog/make-money-with-ai/16-print-on-demand-etsy/)
- Generic print-on-demand apparel struggles on Etsy, especially once ad costs are counted. — [Penluma](https://penluma.com/blog/make-money-with-ai/16-print-on-demand-etsy/)
- Etsy explicitly allows AI-assisted custom pet portraits and print-on-demand on original designs, provided they are disclosed (see Q1). — [Etsy House Rules](https://www.etsy.com/new-guidelines)

### Inferences
**Likely best ratio**
1. Ace-branded original sticker sheets and phone wallpapers or digital art packs, sold through Etsy and a link in bio.
2. Print-on-demand mugs, blankets and tees with an original Ace or "tuxedo cat" design. The design is reusable across products, and Printify or Printful handle fulfilment, with production-partner disclosure.
3. A semi-custom "your cat in Ace's style" portrait. It is AI-assisted but hand-finished, disclosed, and priced in the US$40-80 digital range or bundled with a print or canvas.
4. Q4 memorial ornaments and prints for "cat mom" gifting.

**Higher effort or lower return**
- Calendars are time-boxed: they sell in Q4 and are dead after January.
- Hand-painted portraits are high value per sale but high effort.

**Avoid**
- Generic AI "cat wall art" printables. There are 15M competitors and compression is heavy.
- Cheap AI pet portraits under $20, which compete with AI apps.
- Any AI pattern, colouring how-to or prompt products, banned since October 2026.
- Listings without a real photo of a physical item.
- Undisclosed AI use.
- Relying on Redbubble or Zazzle royalties.

**Canada-specific:** cross-border shipping of physical goods is easiest through print-on-demand partners with US and Canadian print facilities. Digital products avoid shipping entirely. Neither point was verified in this research.

### Gaps
- No independent, verified income data for cat-specific or pet-portrait shops.
- No data on how AI-disclosed listings perform in Etsy search compared with handmade listings.
- Canadian tax (GST/HST on digital sales, US sales tax collected by Etsy) and shipping costs were not covered.
